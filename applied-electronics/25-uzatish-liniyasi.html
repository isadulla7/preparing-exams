25. Uzatish liniyasi va aks etish: Z∞, tP, Γ va lattice diagramma

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: uzun yoki tez simni uzatish liniyasi sifatida qanday hisoblashni: birinchi kuchlanish sakrashi, tarqalish vaqti tP, aks etish koeffitsienti Γ, lattice diagramma yordamida liniya uchlaridagi kuchlanishni bosqichma-bosqich topish. Bu imtihondagi eng ko'p uchraydigan masala turi (2020-07 va 2020-12 da 7 ballik masala, bazada 10 dan ortiq savol). Manba: C2 slaydlari. O'qish vaqti: 40 daqiqa.

1. Qachon sim "uzatish liniyasi" bo'ladi

24-darsda simni bitta nuqta (RC) deb oldik. Bu sim qisqa va signal sekin bo'lganda to'g'ri. Lekin signal o'zgarishi (fronti) juda tez bo'lsa, sim bo'ylab tarqalish vaqti sezilarli bo'ladi. Shunda sim uzatish liniyasi (transmission line) sifatida hisoblanadi.

  • Qoida: signalning ko'tarilish vaqti (tR) tarqalish vaqti tP dan ancha kichik bo'lsa, liniya modeli kerak.
  • Muhimi frontning tikligi, chastota emas.
  • Misollar: plata yo'lakchalari (PCB), koaksial kabel, lentali kabel (flat cable), o'ralgan juftlik (twisted pair).
Hayotiy o'xshatish: uzun arqonning bir uchini silkitsangiz, "to'lqin" arqon bo'ylab yuguradi, boshqa uchiga yetib, devorga urilib orqaga qaytadi. Elektr signal liniya bo'ylab xuddi shunday yuguradi va uchida "aks etadi" (qaytadi).

2. Liniyaning ikki parametri: Z∞ va tP

  • Xarakteristik impedans Z∞ (o'qiladi "Z cheksiz"): to'lqin liniyada yurganda kuchlanishning tokka nisbati, V/I = Z∞. Bu rezistor emas, lekin to'lqin uchun rezistordek ko'rinadi. Odatiy qiymatlar: 10…1000 Ω (koaksial 47…100 Ω, PCB 10…300 Ω).
  • Tarqalish tezligi vP: yorug'lik tezligining 0.6…0.8 qismi, ya'ni 18…24 sm/ns (c = 3·108 m/s = 30 sm/ns).
  • Tarqalish vaqti tP (propagation time, flight time): signal liniyaning boshidan oxirigacha yetish vaqti.
tP = L / vP     (L = liniya uzunligi)     Z∞ = √(LU/CU)     vP = 1/√(LU·CU)

LU, CU = uzunlik birligiga induktivlik va sig'im. Yo'lakcha ingichka bo'lsa, Z∞ katta; keng bo'lsa (yoki kirishlarga sig'im qo'shilsa) Z∞ kichik.

Misol (001-savol): L = 30 sm, vP = 0.7c. vP = 0.7 · 3·108 = 2.1·108 m/s. tP = 0.3 / 2.1·108 = 1.43 ns. Tezroq usul: 0.7c = 21 sm/ns, tP = 30/21 = 1.43 ns.
Muhim: barqaror holatda (hammasi tinchlangach, DC) yo'qotishsiz liniya oddiy sim, ya'ni bitta tugun. Z∞ faqat o'tish jarayonida (to'lqinlar yurganda) ta'sir qiladi.

4. Birinchi sakrash: VB(0)

Driver 0 dan VA ga o'tgan lahzada liniya oxirida nima borligi hali "ma'lum emas" (to'lqin u yerga yetmagan). Liniya driverga Z∞ rezistordek ko'rinadi. RO va Z∞ kuchlanish bo'luvchisini hosil qiladi (2-dars):

v1 = VB(0) = VA · Z∞ / (RO + Z∞)

Bu birinchi sakrash yoki tushuvchi to'lqin (incident wave). U liniya bo'ylab shakli o'zgarmay yuguradi va tP vaqtdan keyin C ga yetadi.

Diqqat: birinchi sakrash RT ga bog'liq emas (slayddagi savol). U faqat VA, RO, Z∞ ga bog'liq.

5. Aks etish koeffitsienti Γ

To'lqin liniya oxiriga yetganda RT ga uriladi. Agar RT = Z∞ bo'lsa, hech narsa o'zgarmaydi: to'lqin butunlay RT da "yutiladi". Agar RT ≠ Z∞ bo'lsa, bir qismi orqaga qaytadi: aks etgan to'lqin (reflected wave).

ΓT = (RT − Z∞) / (RT + Z∞)     qaytgan to'lqin = ΓT · kelgan to'lqin

Driver tomonida ham xuddi shunday (qaytgan to'lqin driverga urilganda):

ΓD = (RO − Z∞) / (RO + Z∞)

Har bir nuqtadagi kuchlanish = o'sha nuqtadan o'tgan barcha to'lqinlarning algebraik yig'indisi.

TerminatsiyaΓNima bo'ladi
Ochiq (RT → ∞)+1Xuddi shunday to'lqin qaytadi, uchda v1 + v1 = 2·v1
Qisqa tutashuv (RT = 0)−1Teskari to'lqin qaytadi, uchda 0
Moslangan (RT = Z∞)0Hech narsa qaytmaydi (adapted, matched)
RT = 2·Z∞+1/3(2Z − Z)/(2Z + Z)
RT = Z∞/2−1/3(Z/2 − Z)/(Z/2 + Z)

Qoida: R > Z∞ → Γ musbat (bir xil ishorali qaytadi), R < Z∞ → Γ manfiy (teskari qaytadi).

6. Lattice diagramma: to'lqinlarni sanash usuli

To'lqin bir uchdan ikkinchisiga borib-qaytaveradi. Buni chizish uchun lattice diagramma (panjara diagramma) ishlatiladi: chapda driver uchi, o'ngda terminatsiya uchi, vaqt pastga qarab o'tadi.

Retsept:

  1. v1 = VA · Z∞ / (RO + Z∞)
  2. v2 = ΓT · v1 (terminatsiyadan qaytgan)
  3. v3 = ΓD · v2 (driverdan qaytgan)
  4. v4 = ΓT · v3, v5 = ΓD · v4, …

Kuchlanishlar:

  • Driver uchi (B) o'zgaradi: 0, 2tP, 4tP, … paytlarda. VB = v1 (0…2tP), keyin v1 + v2 + v3 (2tP…4tP), keyin + v4 + v5, …
  • Terminatsiya uchi (C) o'zgaradi: tP, 3tP, 5tP, … paytlarda. VC = 0 (t < tP), v1 + v2 (tP…3tP), v1 + v2 + v3 + v4 (3tP…5tP), …
  • O'rtadagi nuqtalar ikki baravar ko'p o'zgaradi (to'lqinlar turli paytda o'tadi).
  • Barqaror holat (t → ∞): oddiy kuchlanish bo'luvchi, Z∞ umuman qatnashmaydi:
Vbarq = VA · RT / (RO + RT)     (ochiq uch: Vbarq = VA)
Formulani qisqa yozish (2020-12 rasmiy yechimidagi kabi). U = v1 bo'lsin. Terminatsiya uchida:
tP da: (1 + ΓT)·U
3tP da: (1 + ΓT + ΓT·ΓD + ΓT²·ΓD)·U
5tP da: (1 + ΓT + ΓTΓD + ΓT²ΓD + ΓT²ΓD² + ΓT³ΓD²)·U
Har safar ikkita yangi had qo'shiladi: oldingi oxirgi hadni ΓD ga, keyin ΓT ga ko'paytirasiz.

7. Slayddagi mashq C2.1 va C2.2 (to'liq yechim)

C2.1 sharti: uchi ochiq liniyaga sakrash beriladi. Driver: RO = 160 Ω, 5 V. Liniya: Z∞ = 80 Ω, tP = 10 ns. Driver va terminatsiya uchidagi signallarni chizing.

Γ lar: ΓT = +1 (ochiq). ΓD = (160 − 80)/(160 + 80) = 80/240 = +1/3.

To'lqinlar: v1 = 5 · 80/240 = 1.667 V; v2 = 1 · 1.667 = 1.667 V; v3 = 1/3 · 1.667 = 0.556 V; v4 = 0.556 V; v5 = 0.185 V; v6 = 0.185 V …

Ikkala Γ musbat, shuning uchun hamma to'lqin bir xil ishorali: kuchlanish monoton zinapoya bo'lib 5 V ga yaqinlashadi.

C2.2 sharti: shu sxemaga ikki qabul qilgich ulang: biri driver chiqishida (B), biri liniya oxirida (C). VIL = 1 V, VIH = 3 V. tTX va tK ni toping.

B dagi qabul qilgich: VB(0) = 1.67 V > VIL, demak eng erta sezish darhol: tTXmin = 0. Lekin 1.67 < VIH, demak VIH faqat 2tP da (3.89 V) oshadi: tTXmax = 2tP = 20 ns. Skew tK = 20 ns.

C dagi qabul qilgich: tP gacha 0, tP da 3.33 V (ham VIL, ham VIH dan yuqori). tTXmin = tTXmax = tP = 10 ns. Skew tK = 0.

Ikkala qabul qilgich orasidagi umumiy skew: eng kech (20 ns) − eng erta (0) = 20 ns.

tP va tTX farqi (slayddagi savol): tP = to'lqinning liniya bo'ylab uchish vaqti (faqat uzunlik va tezlikka bog'liq). tTX = qabul qilgich yangi holatni sezgunicha vaqt; u tP ga, aks etishlarga, chegaralarga va joyga bog'liq. Masalan B da tTXmax = 2tP, C da tTX = tP.

8. Imtihon test savollari (asl matn bilan)

026. The reflection coefficient of an open termination of a transmission line is: a) 0   b) 0.5   c) +1   d) -1

Javob

c) +1. Γ = (∞ − Z∞)/(∞ + Z∞) = 1.

038. A CMOS driver with supply voltage Val and output resistance Ro drives a line with characteristic impedance Z∞ terminated on an open circuit. The first voltage step (0→1) at the driver exit has the amplitude: a) Val·Ro/Z∞   b) Val·Z∞/(Ro+Z∞)   c) Val·Ro/(Ro+Z∞)   d) Val

Javob

b) Val·Z∞/(Ro+Z∞). Birinchi sakrash RO va Z∞ bo'luvchisi (ochiq uch hali "ko'rinmaydi").

021. A driver with the output resistance Ro = 2 Z∞ drives at high level (Vdd) a transmission line with the characteristic impedance Z∞ with an adapted termination. The steady-state voltage at the termination end is: a) Vdd / 3   b) 0   c) 2 ∙ Vdd   d) Vdd / 2

Javob

a) Vdd/3. "Adapted termination" = RT = Z∞. Barqaror holat = bo'luvchi: Vdd · Z∞ / (2Z∞ + Z∞) = Vdd/3.

057. A driver with the output resistance Ro = Z∞ drives at high level (Vdd) a line with Z∞ characteristic impedance with an adapted termination. In steady state, the voltage at the termination end is: a) Vdd / 3   b) 0   c) 2 ∙ Vdd   d) Vdd / 2

Javob

d) Vdd/2. Vdd · Z∞ / (Z∞ + Z∞) = Vdd/2.

022. A driver with the output resistance RO = Z∞ drives at high level (VOH) a transmission line with the characteristic impedance Z∞, with open termination. At the end of the transient period, the voltage at the termination end is: a) VOH   b) 0   c) 2 ∙ VOH   d) VOH / 2

Javob

a) VOH. Ochiq uchda barqaror tok yo'q, RO da kuchlanish tushmaydi, butun liniya VOH. (Diqqat: 2·VOH faqat bir lahzalik bo'lishi mumkin, barqaror emas.)

011. A driver with output resistance Ro = Z∞ drives at high level a line with characteristic impedance Z∞ with an open termination and propagation time tp. The transient ends on the entire line: a) after tp   b) infinite time   c) after 2 tp   d) after 3 tp

Javob

c) after 2 tp. v1 = VOH/2. tP da ochiq uchdan v2 = +VOH/2 qaytadi (uchda VOH). 2tP da v2 driverga yetadi: ΓD = 0, hech narsa qaytmaydi. Endi butun liniya VOH, jarayon tugadi.

2021-07 test. The steady state voltage on a transmission line: (a) Can depend on the output resistance of the driver. (b) Does not depend on the termination resistance of the line. (c) Is independent on the output resistance of the driver. (d) Depends on the characteristic impedance of the line, Z∞.

Javob

(a). Vbarq = VA·RT/(RO + RT): RT orqali barqaror tok bo'lsa, RO ga bog'liq. RT ga ham bog'liq (b noto'g'ri). Z∞ ga hech qachon bog'liq emas (d noto'g'ri). (c) har doim emas.

001. A driver powered at 3.3 V and with RO = 70 Ω drives a connection with Z∞ = 60 Ω, propagation speed U = 0.7 c, length d = 30 cm, and open at the remote end. The receivers are CMOS circuits with VIL = 0.8 V and VIH = 2 V. All questions refer to the L-H (low-to-high level) transition. Determine the amplitude of the first step and the propagation time Tp.

Javob

VB(0) = 3.3 · 60/(70 + 60) = 1.52 V. tP = 0.3 m / (0.7 · 3·108 m/s) = 1.43 ns.

O'zingizni tekshiring:
  1. RO = 25 Ω, Z∞ = 75 Ω, VA = 4 V, RT = 225 Ω. v1, ΓD, ΓT, v2, v3 va Vbarq ni toping.
  2. Liniya uzunligi 48 sm, vP = 24 sm/ns. tP = ?
  3. Qisqa tutashgan uchda qaytgan to'lqin qanday?
Javoblar
  1. v1 = 4·75/100 = 3 V. ΓD = (25 − 75)/100 = −0.5. ΓT = (225 − 75)/300 = +0.5. v2 = 1.5 V. v3 = −0.5·1.5 = −0.75 V. Vbarq = 4·225/250 = 3.6 V. (Tekshiruv: uchda tP da 4.5 V, 3tP da 4.5 − 0.75 − 0.375 = 3.375 V, 3.6 ga yaqinlashadi.)
  2. tP = 48/24 = 2 ns.
  3. Γ = −1: kelgan to'lqinga teng, lekin teskari ishorali; uchda kuchlanish 0.
