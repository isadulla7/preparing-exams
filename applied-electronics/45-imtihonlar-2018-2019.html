45. To'liq imtihonlar: 2018–2019 (yozma, 1 soat 30 daqiqa)

Bu sahifa nima uchun: 2018–2019 yillardagi 5 ta yozma imtihonni bitta joyga yig'ish. Har imtihonning tarkibi, ballari, asl shartlari (inglizcha), qisqa yechimi va to'liq yechim turgan dars ko'rsatilgan. Manba: 201803, 201902, 201906-a/b, 201907 correction.pdf (qo'lda yozilgan rasmiy yechimlar). Har imtihon uchun 90 daqiqa ajrating.

Sizda laboratoriya bali yo'q, demak bunday imtihonda ko'pi bilan 27 ball olasiz va 18 ball uchun yozma qismning 2/3 qismini to'g'ri yechishingiz kerak. Imtihon 2018-03 (Special Call 2018)

Exercise 1 (9 ball): SAR ADC

A successive approximations 8 bit A/D converter has a clock of 1 MHz and an input range of 10 V. If at the input there is a voltage of 4.8 V and a conversion is started compute: 1) The sequence of values at the output of the internal D/A converter as a function of the time (7 points) 2) The number of cycles required for the conversion (1 point) 3) The final digital output voltage (1 point)
Yechim

DAC: 5 → 2.5 → 3.75 → 4.375 → 4.6875 → 4.84375 → 4.765625 → 4.8046875 V, har biri 1 µs. Bitlar: 0 1 1 1 1 0 1 0. Taktlar: 8. Kod: 0111 1010 (122 → 4.766 V).

Rasmiy varaqda 0111 1011 yozilgan, lekin oxirgi DAC qiymati 4.805 V > 4.8 V bo'lgani uchun oxirgi bit 0 bo'lishi kerak. Jadvalni to'liq ko'rsatsangiz ball olasiz. To'liq jadval: 20-dars.

Exercise 2 (5 ball): SNR va minimal namuna olish chastotasi

A triangular signal with 5 kHz frequency is digitalized with a 12 bit AD converter, with an input range of 5 V. 1. Find the SNR when the input signal has peak to peak amplitude of 2.5 V (2 points) 2. If the spectrum of the signal may be considered 0 after the 8th harmonic which is the minimum sampling frequency (3 points)
Yechim

(1) Uchburchak: 6·12 = 72 dB. Signal diapazonning yarmi → −20·lg(5/2.5) = −6 dB. SNR = 66 dB.

(2) 8-garmonika: 8 · 5 = 40 kHz = signal kengligi. Nyquist: fs ≥ 80 kHz. (16b-dars, 16a-dars)

Exercise 3 (8 ball): DATA va CLK liniyalari

Given the schematic of the figure, where the parameters of the transmission lines are: L1 = 35 cm, L2 = 20 cm, v = c/3. The input signals are DATA = 3.3 u(t) and CLK = 3.3 u(t − t0) and the parameters of the flip-flops are the threshold voltage VT = 1.7 V and set-up time = 2 ns. Determine the minimum value of t0 which will guarantee the correct transmission of the data.
Yechim

v = 10 sm/ns. DATA: R0 = Z∞ (RWS), ochiq uchda 3.3 V → tDATA = 3.5 ns. CLK: oxiri moslashgan (IWS) → tCLK = 2 ns. Shart: tCLK + t0 ≥ tDATA + tsetup → t0 ≥ 3.5 + 2 − 2 = 3.5 ns. Sxema rasmi bilan: 29-dars.

Exercise 4 (8 ball): buck

Draw the schematic of a buck (step-down) power converter (5 points). Compute the the value of the output voltage as a function of the switching frequency, duty cycle and input voltage (3 points).
Yechim
  1. Sxema: VI → kalit → tugun → L → VU (CU ‖ yuklama). Tugundan yerga diod (katodi tugunga qaragan). Kirishda CI.
  2. T1 (kalit ON): VL = VI − VU, tok o'sadi: ΔI = (VI − VU)·T1/L.
  3. T2 (kalit OFF, diod o'tkazadi): VL = −VU, tok kamayadi: ΔI = VU·T2/L.
  4. Tenglashtiramiz: (VI − VU)·T1 = VU·T2 → VI·T1 = VU·(T1 + T2) → VU = VI·D.
  5. Rasmiy yechimdagi xulosa: "VU does not depend on the frequency": chiqish faqat D va VI ga bog'liq.

Sxema rasmi: 33-dars; keltirib chiqarish usuli: 34-dars, 7-bo'lim.

Imtihon 2019-02 (Winter Call 2019)

Exercise 1 (14 ball): SAR ADC va SNR

A successive approximations 7 bit A/D converter has a clock of 2 MHz and an input range from 0 V to 5 V. If at the input there is a constant voltage of 2.6 V and a conversion is started compute: 1) The sequence of values at the output of the internal D/A converter as a function of the time (7 points) 2) The number of cycles required for the conversion (1 point) 3) The final digital output voltage (1 point). If the converter is used to sample and convert the values of a sinusoidal waveform with frequency f0, average value of 3 V and peak to peak value of 2.5 V which are 4. The maximum frequency f0 of the signal (2 points) 5. The Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) in dB associated to the conversion (3 points)
Yechim

(1) DAC: 2.5 → 3.75 → 3.125 → 2.8125 → 2.65625 → 2.578125 → 2.6171875 V (har biri 0.5 µs). (2) 7 takt. (3) Kod 1000010. (4) fc = 2 MHz/7 = 285.7 kHz → f0 ≤ 142.9 kHz. (5) Sinus: 6·7 + 1.76 − 6 = 37.8 dB (rasmiy varaqda 37.6). O'rtacha qiymat (3 V) SNR ga ta'sir qilmaydi. (20-dars)

Exercise 2 (11 ball): ikki qabul qilgich, t0

Given the schematic of the figure, where the parameters of the transmission lines are: L1 = 35 cm, L2 = 20 cm, L3 = 30 cm, L4 = 30 cm, v = c/3, R0 = 100 Ω, where the input signals are DATA1 = 3.3 u(t), DATA2 = 3.3 u(t) and CLK = 3.3 u(t − t0) and the parameters of the flip-flops are the threshold voltage VT = 2.4 V and set-up time tsetup = 2 ns, determine the minimum value of t0 which will guarantee the correct transmission of the data to both flip flops.
Yechim

tD1 = 3.5 ns, tCK1 = 2 ns. DATA2 (generator 2R0, ΓG = 1/3): 3 ns da 2.2 V < 2.4 V, 9 ns da 2.93 V → tD2 = 9 ns; tCK2 = 3 ns. REC1: t0 ≥ 3.5 ns; REC2: t0 ≥ 9 + 2 − 3 = 8 ns → t0,min = 8 ns. (29-dars)

Exercise 3 (5 ball): boost sxemasi

Draw the schematic of a boost (step-up) power converter.
Yechim

Vin → Cin (yerga) → L → tugun → diod (anodi tugunda) → Vout, chiqishda Cout ‖ yuklama. Kalit (S) tugundan yerga. Eslab qolish: boost da L kirish tomonda, kalit yerga, diod chiqishga qarab. Formula: Vout = Vin/(1 − D). (34-dars)

Imtihon 2019-06, A varianti (1st Summer Call 2019)

Exercise 1 (9 ball): SAR ADC

1. Draw the schematic of a 8-bit Successive approximations A/D converter (1 point) 2. If the input range is 0 V ÷ 10 V and the clock frequency is 2 MHz compute the maximum conversion time (1 point) 3. Which is the maximum bandwidth of the signal to be converted (1 point) 4. If the input signal has the value of 2.8 V compute the sequence of values as a function of time at the output of internal DA converter (4 points) 5. If the input signal is a triangular waveform with peak-to-peak value of 5 V and average value of 2.5 V which is the SNR associated to the AD conversion? (2 points)
Yechim

Sxema: komparator (Vin va DAC), SAR registr, DAC teskari aloqada. Tc = 8/2 MHz = 4 µs; fc = 250 kHz → kenglik ≤ 125 kHz. 2.8 V: 5, 2.5, 3.75, 3.125, 2.8125, 2.65625, 2.734375, 2.7734375 → kod 01000111. SNR = 48 − 6 = 42 dB. (20-dars, sxema: 18-dars)

Exercise 2 (6 ball): moslashgan generator

Consider a generator connected to a receiver with a transmission line with characteristic impedance of 150 Ω and propagation speed of 0.7c and a length of 25 cm. Calculate the transmission time tTX for a L→H transition and the skew (tK) given the following parameters: Generator: open circuit voltages VOH = 4 V, VOL = 0 V, RG = 150 Ω. Receiver: infinite input impedance, VIL = 1 V, VIH = 2.4 V.
Yechim

RG = Z∞ → liniyaga 2 V kiradi, ochiq uchda 4 V bo'ladi va ikkala chegaradan bir vaqtda o'tadi. v = 21 sm/ns → tTX = 25/21 = 1.19 ns, tK = 0 (ikkala chegara bir vaqtda kesiladi). (29-dars)

Exercise 3 (6 ball): buck-boost formulasini keltirib chiqarish

Draw the schematic of a buck-boost converter (1 point). Which is the control variable of the converter? (1 point). Derive (that is not just writing the result) the value of the input/output transfer function as a function of the control variable assuming that the converter is operating in CCM (4 points)
Yechim

Boshqaruvchi kattalik: kalitni boshqaruvchi signalning duty cycle D si. T1: VL = VI → ΔI = VI·T1/L. T2: VL = VU → ΔI = −VU·T2/L. Tenglab: VU/VI = −T1/T2 = −D/(1 − D) = D/(D − 1). "Not just writing the result": 4 ball keltirib chiqarish uchun. (34-dars, 7-bo'lim)

Exercise 4 (6 ball): fotodiod, noaniqlik

The figure shows a system for the measurement of the illuminance EV through a photodiode and a trans-resistance amplifier. The photodiode is characterized by ISC = S·EV + IOFF where S = (10 ± 0.1) nA/lux and IOFF = (0 ± 50) nA. The resistor RF has a nominal value of 100 kΩ and a relative tolerance of ±1%. Estimate value and uncertainty of the illuminance EV when Vout = (0.734 ± 0.005) V.
Yechim

EV = Vout/(RF·S) − IOFF/S = 734 lux. Hissalar: 5.00 + 7.34 + 7.34 + 5.00 = 24.7 lux → EV = (734 ± 25) lux. (39-dars)

Imtihon 2019-07 (2nd Summer Call 2019)

Exercise 1 (6 ball): SAR ADC

1. Draw the schematic of a 8-bit Successive approximations A/D converter (1 point) 2. If the input range is 0 V ÷ 10 V and the clock frequency is 2 MHz compute the maximum conversion time (2 points) 3. Which is the maximum bandwidth of the signal to be converted (1 point) 4. If the input signal is a triangular waveform with peak-to-peak value of 5 V and average value of 2.5 V which is the SNR associated to the AD conversion? (2 points)
Yechim

Tc,max = 8 · 0.5 µs = 4 µs; fc = 250 kHz → ≤ 125 kHz; SNR = 6·8 − 20·lg(10/5) = 42 dB. (20-dars)

Exercise 2 (11 ball): tTX, skew, Z0 va v0

A printed circuit board of length L = 27 cm can be considered as a transmission line with a characteristic impedance Z0 = 150 Ω and propagation speed v0 = 0.5c. 1) If a signal x(t) = 3.3 V u(t) is provided at one end by a generator of impedance 300 Ω and at the other end there is a logic gate with infinite input impedance and logic thresholds VIL = 0.9 V and VIH = 2.7 V compute the transmission time and the skew of the signal over the line (8 points) 2) If the capacitance per unit length of the line increases by a factor of 4 how do change the values of Z0 and v0? (3 points)
Yechim

tP = 27/15 = 1.8 ns. ΓG = +1/3, boshlang'ich to'lqin 1.1 V. 1.8 ns da 2.2 V (VIL dan o'tdi, VIH dan yo'q); 5.4 ns da 2.93 V > 2.7 V. tTX = 5.4 ns, tK = 3.6 ns. C ×4 → Z0 = 75 Ω, v0 = 0.25c. (29-dars)

Exercise 3 (4 ball): buck-boost sxemasi

Draw the schematic of a buck-boost converter (2 point). Which is the control variable of the converter? (2 point)
Yechim

VI → kalit → tugun; tugundan L yerga; tugundan diod (teskari yo'nalishda) → VU, chiqishda CU ‖ yuklama. Chiqish qutbi teskari. Boshqaruvchi kattalik: duty cycle D. (34-dars)

Exercise 4 (6 ball): rezistiv harorat sensori

Rθ = R0·(1 + A·θ), R0 = 100 Ω, A = 0.004 (°C)⁻¹, δθ = 0.6 °C + 0.01·|θ|. RF = 1 kΩ ± 0.2%, VS = 1 V ± 0.5 mV. Estimate value and uncertainty of the temperature θ when VF = (0.8800 ± 0.0005) V.
Yechim

θ = (1/A)·(RFVS/(R0VF) − RF/R0 − 1) = 90.9 °C. Hissalar 0.68 + 1.42 + 1.61 + 1.51 → θ = (90.9 ± 5.2) °C. Rasmiy varaq faqat "2019-06 B yechimiga qarang" deydi, raqamlar shu usul bilan hisoblangan. (39-dars)

Yozma imtihonda ball olish qoidalari

  • Sxema so'ralsa, aniq chizing va elementlarni nomlang (VI, L, D, C, kalit S). Sxema 1–5 ball beradi va eng oson ball.
  • "Derive" so'ralsa, faqat javobni yozmang: T1 va T2 dagi VL, ΔI tengligi, D ni qo'yish.
  • SAR ketma-ketligi: jadval (takt, DAC qiymati, solishtirish, bit) chizing. 7 balldan ko'pi shu jadval uchun.
  • Liniya masalasi: avval tP, ΓG, ΓL, boshlang'ich to'lqinni yozing, keyin lattice diagramma.
  • Noaniqlik: model → kalibrovka → qiymat → sezgirliklar → hissalar → natija (qiymat ± noaniqlik, birligi bilan).
