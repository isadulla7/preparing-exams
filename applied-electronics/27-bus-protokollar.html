27. Bus protokollari: sinxron va asinxron yozish sikli

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: skew qabul qilgichdagi setup va hold vaqtini qanday "yeydi"; ma'lumotni simlar orqali to'g'ri yozish uchun ikkita asosiy usul: sinxron sikl (qat'iy kutish vaqtlari) va asinxron sikl (javob signali ACK bilan, "handshake"). Bazadagi 6 ta savol (015, 031, 096, 098, 110, 113) shu formulalar bilan yechiladi. Manba: C3 oxiri va C4 slaydlari. O'qish vaqti: 30 daqiqa.

1. Nega protokol kerak

Bitta bit emas, bir guruh bit (bayt, so'z) bir nechta parallel sim orqali yuboriladi. Qabul qilgich tomonda bu bitlar registrga (D flip-flop lar, 7-dars) yoziladi. Registrga yozish uchun takt kerak; bu takt signali bus da STB (strobe, "yozib ol" buyrug'i) deyiladi. Ma'lumot signali INF (information) deyiladi.

8-darsdan: flip-flop to'g'ri ishlashi uchun ma'lumot takt frontidan tSU oldin tayyor bo'lishi va tH keyin ham turishi kerak. Aks holda metastabillik (xato holat) bo'ladi.

Muammo: INF va STB turli simlardan keladi, ularning har birida tTX har xil (24–26-darslar, skew tK). Shuning uchun manba tomonda yaxshi ko'ringan vaqt munosabati qabul qilgich tomonda buziladi.

Hayotiy o'xshatish: siz do'stingizga ikkita xabar yuborasiz: avval "manzil: …", keyin "endi yoz". Agar xabarlar turli yo'llardan borib, ikkinchisi birinchisidan oldin yetsa, do'stingiz noto'g'ri manzilni yozib oladi. Shuning uchun ikkinchi xabarni biroz kutib yuborish kerak, kutish vaqti yo'llardagi farqdan (skew) katta bo'lishi lozim.

2. Skew setup vaqtini kamaytiradi

Manbada INF t0 da, STB tSU(D) keyin chiqariladi. Eng yomon holat: INF eng kech keladi (tTXmin + tK), STB eng erta keladi (tTXmin). Qabul qilgichda haqiqiy setup:

tSU(R) = tSU(D) − tK

Ya'ni qabul qilgich tomonda setup zaxirasi skew qadar qisqaradi. Hold uchun ham xuddi shunday. Demak manba kutish vaqtlariga tK ni qo'shishi kerak.

3. Sinxron yozish sikli (fixed timing)

Hamma vaqtlarni manba boshqaradi, qabul qilgichdan javob kutilmaydi. Ketma-ketlik:

  1. INF ni chiqarish.
  2. tA kutish (setup ni ta'minlash uchun): tA ≥ tSU + tK.
  3. STB ni ko'tarish (qabul qilgich registriga takt).
  4. tB kutish (hold ni ta'minlash uchun): tB ≥ tH + tK. Bu vaqt davomida STB yuqori turadi.
  5. INF va STB ni olib tashlash.
tWR (sinxron) = tA + tB = tSU + tH + 2·tK

Muhim: sikl davomiyligi tTXmax ga bog'liq emas, faqat skew ga bog'liq. Kamchilik: manba qabul qilgichning tSU, tH va tK ni oldindan bilishi va eng yomon holatga (eng sekin qabul qilgichga) moslashishi kerak.

4. Asinxron yozish sikli (handshake bilan)

Vaqtni ikki tomon birga boshqaradi. Qabul qilgich "yozib oldim" degan javob signali ACK (acknowledge) yuboradi. STB va ACK ning bu "qo'l berib ko'rishishi" handshake deyiladi.

  1. Manba INF ni chiqaradi va faqat tK kutadi (INF STB dan oldin yetishi uchun).
  2. STB ni ko'taradi.
  3. STB qabul qilgichga yetadi (tTX). Qabul qilgich o'zi tSU va tH ni o'tkazib, registrga yozadi va ACK ni ko'taradi.
  4. ACK manbaga yetadi (tTX). Manba INF va STB ni olib tashlaydi.
  5. STB ning tushishi qabul qilgichga yetadi (tTX), u ACK ni tushiradi; bu manbaga yetadi (tTX). Sikl tugadi.
tWR (asinxron) = tK + tSU + tH + 4·tTXmax

Afzallik: manba faqat tK ni bilishi kerak, sikl qabul qilgich tezligiga o'zi moslashadi. Kamchilik: 4·tTXmax qo'shiladi, shuning uchun odatda sekinroq.

O'qish sikli (slayd, qisqacha): ma'lumot qabul qilgichdan (slave) master ga keladi, shuning uchun so'rov borib, ma'lumot qaytadi. Sinxron o'qish: tRD = tA + tSU + tH + 4tTXmax. Asinxron o'qish: tRD = tK + t1 + t2 + 4tTXmax (t1 = setup + hold, t2 = ma'lumot manbaining kirish vaqti). Eng yuqori tezlik: source-synchronous protokol (STB ni ma'lumot manbai o'zi yuboradi, masalan DRAM dagi SSTL); sikl davomiyligi faqat tK ga bog'liq.

5. Imtihon savollari (asl matn bilan)

113. In a synchronous write cycle, the STB signal must remain high for a minimum delay of: a) Tsu + Tk   b) Th + Tk   c) Tk   d) Tk + Ttxmin

Javob

b) Th + Tk. STB ko'tarilgach manba tB kutadi, bu vaqt hold ni ta'minlaydi: tB ≥ tH + tK.

015. A driver powered at 3.3 V and with RO = 70 Ω drives a connection with Z∞ = 60 Ω, propagation speed U = 0.7 c, length d = 30 cm, and open at the remote end. The receivers are CMOS circuits with VIL = 0.8 V and VIH = 2 V. All questions refer to the L-H (low-to-high level) transition. This connection is used on a parallel bus with a synchronous protocol. The registers of receiver have a set-up time TSU = 5 ns and hold time TH = 2 ns. Trace the STB (strobe) and DATA signals at the source and at the destination for a write cycle, and determine the minimum cycle duration (assume that the logic circuits have zero delays).

Javob

Bu 001/082 dagi liniya. 082 dan: driver yonidagi qabul qilgich uchun TK = 2.86 ns (eng yomon skew).

TCY = TSU + 2·TK + TH = 5 + 2·2.86 + 2 = 12.7 ns. Chizma yuqoridagi sinxron sikl rasmi kabi.

031. A driver powered at 3.3 V (VOH = 3.3 V, VOL = 0 V) drives a transmission line with Z∞ = 50 Ω, propagation speed U = 0.7 c, length d = 20 cm, open at the far end. The receivers are CMOS circuits with VIL = 1 V and VIH = 2 V. This line is used on a parallel bus with synchronous protocol. The registers (D flip-flops) of the receiver have set-up time TSU = 4 ns and hold time TH = 1 ns. Draw the STB and DATA signals at the source and at the destination for a write cycle, and determine the minimum duration of the cycle (assume logic circuits with zero delays between their inputs and outputs).

Javob

Bu 044 dagi liniya (RO = 80 Ω): driver yonida TK = 2tP = 2 ns.

Tcycle = 2·TK + TSU + TH = 4 + 4 + 1 = 9 ns.

110. A driver is connected to a transmission line at point B in the figure. The line is 10 cm long, has Z∞ = 100 Ω, and the propagation speed is 0.7 c. Parameters Driver: Voh = 3.0 V; Vol = 0 V; Vcc = 3.0 V (Roh and Rol are negligible) Receiver: Vih = 2 V; Vil = 0.5 V. The line is used in a bus with a synchronous transmission protocol. Assuming incident wave switching, determine the duration of a write cycle if the receiver setup time is 2 ns and the hold time is 1 ns.

Javob

IWS → har bir qabul qilgich birinchi to'lqinda o'tadi, TK = 0 (rasmiy yechim).

tWR = 2·0 + 2 + 1 = 3 ns.

096. A driver is connected to a transmission line as in the figure (024 dagi sxema). The line is 10 cm long, has Z∞ = 75 Ω, and the propagation speed is 0.7 c. The termination resistance RT is adapted. Parameters Driver: Voh = 3.0 V; Vol = 0 V; Vcc = 3.0 V (Roh and Rol negligible) Receiver: Vih = 2 V; Vil = 0.5 V. The line is used in a bus with an asynchronous transmission protocol. Determine the duration of a cycle if the setup time of the receiver is 2 ns and the hold time is 1 ns.

Javob

tP = 10 sm / 21 sm/ns = 0.48 ns. IWS (024 da RS tanlangan), TK = 0, tTXmax = tP.

tWR = TK + TSU + TH + 4·tP = 0 + 2 + 1 + 4·0.48 = 4.92 ns.

098. A driver with output impedance RS is connected to a transmission line as in the figure (058 dagi sxema). The line is 20 cm long, has Z∞ = 80 Ω, and the propagation speed is 0.5 c. Parameters: Driver: VOH = 3.0 V; VOL = 0.3 V; VCC = 3.3 V (supply voltage) Receiver: VIH = 2.2 V; VIL = 1.4 V. The line is used in a bus with an asynchronous transmission protocol. Determine the duration of a write cycle if the setup time is 2 ns and the hold time is 1 ns.

Javob

0.5c = 15 sm/ns, tP = 20/15 = 1.33 ns. 058 da RS IWS uchun tanlangan: TK = 0, tTXmax = tP.

tWR = 0 + 2 + 1 + 4·1.33 = 8.33 ns.

Imtihon qoidasi: masalada "IWS" deyilsa yoki RS IWS uchun tanlangan bo'lsa, TK = 0 va tTXmax = tP oling. Aks holda TK ni 26-darsdagi usul bilan toping (odatda driver yonidagi qabul qilgich uchun TK = 2tP yoki shunga yaqin). Keyin sinxron yoki asinxron formulaga qo'ying.
O'zingizni tekshiring:
  1. tSU = 3 ns, tH = 1 ns, tK = 1.5 ns. Sinxron sikl uchun tA, tB va tWR?
  2. Xuddi shu qiymatlar va tTXmax = 2 ns bilan asinxron sikl?
  3. Nega sinxron sikl tTX ga bog'liq emas?
Javoblar
  1. tA ≥ 4.5 ns, tB ≥ 2.5 ns, tWR = 3 + 1 + 3 = 7 ns.
  2. 1.5 + 3 + 1 + 8 = 13.5 ns.
  3. INF va STB ikkalasi ham bir xil yo'nalishda, bir xil o'rtacha kechikish bilan boradi; ularning nisbiy holatini faqat farq (tK) buzadi. Javob kutilmaydi, shuning uchun borib-kelish vaqti qo'shilmaydi.

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

tSU(R) = tSU(D) − tK Sinxron: tA ≥ tSU + tK, tB ≥ tH + tK  ·  tWR = tSU + tH + 2tK Asinxron: tWR = tK + tSU + tH + 4·tTXmax
  • Sinxron: manba hammasini boshqaradi, tTX ga bog'liq emas, eng yomon holatga moslanadi.
  • Asinxron: ACK bilan handshake, manba faqat tK ni biladi, 4 marta sim orqali o'tish.
  • STB yuqori turishi kerak: tB ≥ tH + tK (113).
  • IWS bo'lsa TK = 0, tTXmax = tP.
  • Javoblar: 113 → b; 015 → 12.7 ns; 031 → 9 ns; 110 → 3 ns; 096 → 4.92 ns; 098 → 8.33 ns.
