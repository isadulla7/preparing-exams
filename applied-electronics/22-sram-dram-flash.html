22. Xotira katakchalari: DRAM, SRAM, DDR va Flash

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: bitta bit ichkarida qanday saqlanishini: DRAM (1 tranzistor + kondensator), SRAM (6 tranzistor), Flash (suzuvchi zatvorli tranzistor). Ularning farqlari, DRAM ni o'qish va yangilash (refresh), DDR tezligi, NAND va NOR flash. Bu B mavzu: test savollari deyarli har imtihonda: 036, 106, 089, 032, 100, 037, 2021-06 va 2021-07 testlari. Manba: B7 slaydlari va B7e mashqlari. O'qish vaqti: 35 daqiqa.

1. DRAM: bit = kondensatordagi zaryad

DRAM (dynamic RAM) katakchasi: 1 ta MOS tranzistor + 1 ta kondensator CS. Zaryadlangan = 1, bo'sh = 0. Tranzistor (pass-transistor) wordline bilan yoqilib, kondensatorni bitline ga ulaydi.

  • Yozish: bitline ga 1 yoki 0 kuchlanish beriladi, WL yoqiladi → CS shu kuchlanishgacha zaryadlanadi/bo'shaydi.
  • O'qish: (1) bitline oldindan VDD/2 ga zaryadlanadi; (2) WL yoqiladi, CS va bitline sig'imi CBL zaryadni bo'lishadi; (3) bitline biroz oshadi (1) yoki kamayadi (0); (4) sense amplifier bu kichik farqni to'liq 0/1 ga kuchaytiradi; (5) o'qish kondensatorni "buzadi", shuning uchun bit qayta yoziladi.
  • Refresh (yangilash): kondensator sizish toki bilan asta bo'shaydi → davriy ravishda "soxta o'qish" bilan qayta yoziladi. Shuning uchun "dynamic".
O'xshatish: DRAM katakchasi = teshik stakan. Suv bor = 1, yo'q = 0. Suv asta sizib chiqadi, shuning uchun vaqti-vaqti bilan tekshirib, qayta to'ldirish kerak (refresh). O'qish uchun stakanni katta idishga (bitline) quyasiz: idishdagi sath ozgina ko'tarilsa, stakanda suv bor edi.

106. A DRAM memory cell includes: a) A floating MOS transistor gate   b) A MOS transistor and a capacitance   c) 6 MOS transistors   d) a NMOS transistor, a PMOS transistor, and two capacitances

Javob

b).

2021-06 (test). The voltage of a storage capacitor of a DRAM memory cell is 0 V. During a read operation with the bitline voltage initially set at VDD/2: (a) The bitline voltage decreases by ΔV < VDD/2. (b) The memory cell maintains the same voltage. (c) The bitline voltage decreases by ΔV = VDD/2 − Vth. (d) The bitline voltage increases by ΔV < VDD/2.

Javob

(a). Bo'sh kondensator bitline dan zaryad oladi: bitline VDD/2 dan 0 V tomonga kamayadi, lekin CS ≪ CBL bo'lgani uchun ozgina (ΔV < VDD/2). (b) noto'g'ri: kondensator kuchlanishi o'zgaradi (shuning uchun qayta yoziladi).

B7e Exercise 1: refresh davri (rasmiy yechim)

DRAM cell with leakage current Ileak = 25 fA and CS = 20 fF. Calculate the refresh period to keep the CS voltage variation lower than 0.1 V.

Doimiy tok bilan bo'shash: I = C·ΔV/Δt → T ≤ CS·ΔV/Ileak = 20 fF · 0.1 V / 25 fA = 0.08 s = 80 ms.

2. SRAM: bit = ikki invertor "qulfi"

  • Yozish: BL = 1 (yoki 0), BLc = teskarisi, WL = 1 → invertorlar yangi holatga "ag'dariladi".
  • O'qish: BL va BLc ikkalasi VDD ga oldindan zaryadlanadi, WL = 1 → bittasi pasayadi; differensial sense amplifier qaysi biri pasayganini aniqlaydi. O'qish katakchani buzmaydi.

3. DRAM tezligi: SDRAM va DDR

  • SDRAM (synchronous): buyruqlar takt signalining ko'tarilish chetida.
  • DDR (double data rate): ma'lumot taktning ikkala chetida (ko'tarilish va tushish) uzatiladi → bir taktda 2 so'z → SDRAM dan 2 marta tez. CAS latency kasr bo'lishi mumkin (2.5), chunki yarim takt ham bor.
Tezlik = 2 · fbus · (bit kengligi)

037. A DDR4 memory has the bus clock at 2000 MHz and 8-bit parallelism. The maximum rate is: a) 16 Gbit/s   b) 32 Gbit/s   c) 64 Gbit/s   d) 2 Gbit/s

Javob

b) 32 Gbit/s. 2 · 2000 MHz · 8 bit = 32 000 Mbit/s.

2021-07 (test). All DDR4 DRAM memories transfer at most: (a) 4 bytes on each bus clock cycle. (b) 1 word on each bus clock transition. (c) 2 words on each bus clock transition. (d) 1 word on each bus clock cycle.

Javob

(b). Rasmiy izoh: DDR har takt davrida 2 so'z, ya'ni har bir chetda 1 so'z uzatadi.

4. Flash: suzuvchi zatvor (floating gate)

Floating gate MOS: oddiy gate ostida, hamma tomondan izolyator bilan o'ralgan ikkinchi "suzuvchi" gate bor. Yuqori kuchlanish (≈ 20 V) bilan unga elektronlar "tiqiladi"; kuchlanish olingach ular izolyator ichida yillab qamalib qoladi → tok o'chsa ham saqlanadi (non-volatile).

  • Qamalgan zaryad tranzistorning ochilish chegarasi VTH ni oshiradi: ΔVTH = −Q/CPP. O'qishda gate ga Vread beriladi: tok bor → bir qiymat, tok yo'q → ikkinchisi.
  • EPROM: ultrabinafsha nur bilan o'chiriladi. EEPROM: elektr bilan. Flash: elektr bilan, butun blok birdaniga ("in a flash") o'chiriladi.
  • Yozish (program): bitni 1 → 0. O'chirish (erase): blokdagi hamma bitni 0 → 1. Shuning uchun qayta yozishdan oldin o'chirish kerak; yozish sekin.
  • Har blokni cheklangan marta (masalan 104) o'chirish mumkin → yeyilish (wear) → wear leveling: yozuvlarni hamma bloklarga teng tarqatish.

B7e Exercise 2 va 3 (rasmiy yechimlar)

Ex 2 (saqlash muddati): CPP = 50 aF, ruxsat etilgan ΔVTH = 1 V, 10 yil. ΔQ = CPP·ΔVTH = 50 aC ≈ 313 elektron. I = 50·10−18 C / (10 yil sekundlarda) = 1.6·10−25 A ≈ 0.6 elektron/hafta.

Ex 3 (yeyilish): 4096 blok, har biri 104 marta; har 10 daqiqada 200 blokli fayl. Wear leveling siz: 104 · 10 min = 100 000 min ≈ 69 kun. Wear leveling bilan: × 4096/200 → ≈ 1422 kun (3.9 yil).

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

DRAM = 1 MOS + C  ·  SRAM = 6 MOS  ·  Flash = floating gate MOS ΔVBL = (Vbit − VDD/2)·CS/(CS + CBL)  ·  Trefresh ≤ CS·ΔV/Ileak  ·  DDR: 2 · fbus · bit
  • DRAM o'qish: precharge VDD/2 → zaryad bo'lishish → kichik ΔV (0 bo'lsa kamayadi, ΔV < VDD/2) → sense amp → qayta yozish. Sizish → refresh.
  • SRAM: tez, refresh yo'q, kam zich. DRAM: zich, arzon, sekinroq.
  • DDR: har takt chetida 1 so'z (taktda 2).
  • Flash: blok bo'yicha o'chirish, cheklangan sikllar, wear leveling. NOR = tez o'qish (kod); NAND = zichlik (SD, SSD).
