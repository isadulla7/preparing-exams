23. Mashq: xotira va FPGA (12 ta imtihon savoli)

Bu dars nima uchun: savollar bazasidagi xotira va FPGA ga oid 12 ta savolni bir joyda takrorlash (4 tasi hisob/sxema). Asl inglizcha matn, tarjima va yashirilgan javob. Kerakli darslar: 10, 21, 22. Vaqt: 20 daqiqa.
Usul: har savolga avval o'zingiz javob bering (qog'ozga yozing), keyin "Javob" ni oching. Xato savollarni belgilab, 2 kundan keyin faqat shularni qayta yeching.

036. An SRAM memory cell includes:

  1. A floating gate MOS
  2. A MOS and a capacitor
  3. 6 MOS
  4. a NMOS, a PMOS and two capacitors

Tarjima: SRAM xotira katakchasi nimadan iborat?

Javob

(c) 6 MOS. Ikkita bir-biriga ulangan inverter (4 MOS) va ikkita kirish kaliti (2 MOS): 6 MOS. (22-dars)

106. A DRAM memory cell includes:

  1. A floating MOS transistor gate
  2. A MOS transistor and a capacitance
  3. 6 MOS transistors
  4. a NMOS ttransistor, a PMOS transistor, and two capacitances

Tarjima: DRAM xotira katakchasi nimadan iborat?

Javob

(b) A MOS transistor and a capacitance. Bitta kalit tranzistor va bitta kichik kondensator: zaryad bor = 1, yo'q = 0. (22-dars)

089. A FLASH memory cell includes:

  1. A floating gate MOS
  2. A MOS and a capacitance
  3. 6 MOS
  4. an NMOS, a PMOS and two capacitances

Tarjima: Flash xotira katakchasi nimadan iborat?

Javob

(a) A floating gate MOS. Suzuvchi zatvorga (floating gate) qamalgan zaryad tok o'chganda ham saqlanadi. (22-dars)

032. A FLASH NOR memory has, compared to a NAND:

  1. greater word width
  2. greater reading speed
  3. better noise immunity
  4. greater density

Tarjima: NOR flash NAND flash ga nisbatan qanday?

Javob

(b) greater reading speed. NOR da har katak bitline ga alohida (parallel) ulangan, shuning uchun o'qish tez va har baytga to'g'ridan-to'g'ri murojaat qilinadi. Lekin zichlik pastroq. (22-dars)

100. A FLASH NAND memory has, compared to a NOR:

  1. larger word width
  2. faster reading speed
  3. better noise immunity
  4. greater density

Tarjima: NAND flash NOR flash ga nisbatan qanday?

Javob

(d) greater density. NAND da kataklar ketma-ket zanjirda, kontaktlar kam, shuning uchun zichlik yuqori (arzon, katta hajm: USB, SSD). O'qish sekinroq. (22-dars)

037. A DDR4 memory has the bus clock at 2000 MHz and 8-bit parallelism. The maximum rate is:

  1. 16 Gbit/s
  2. 32 Gbit/s
  3. 64 Gbit/s
  4. 2 Gbit/s

Tarjima: DDR4 xotiraning bus takti 2000 MHz, kengligi 8 bit. Maksimal tezlik qancha?

Javob

(b) 32 Gbit/s. DDR: har taktda 2 ta uzatish → 2 · 2000 MHz = 4000 MT/s. 4000·10⁶ · 8 bit = 32 Gbit/s. (22-dars)

2. Dekoder va so'z chizig'i (wordline) kechikishi

K bit → 2K chiqish → 2K ta K-kirishli NAND (+ inverterlar)t = 0.69·RON·Cwordline, Cwordline = (katak soni)·(katakdagi kalitlar soni)·CG SRAM katagida wordline ga 2 ta kalit ulangan, DRAM da 1 ta.

065. An address decoder with 4-bit input requires:

  1. 4 NAND gates
  2. 8 NAND gates
  3. 4 AND gates
  4. 16 NAND gates

Tarjima: 4 bitli kirishli adres dekoderiga nima kerak?

Javob

(d) 16 NAND gates. 4 bit → 2⁴ = 16 ta chiqish (wordline). Har chiqish uchun bitta 4 kirishli NAND (kirishlarga A yoki Ā beriladi). (21-dars, 5-dars)

002. Consider an SRAM with 16 wordlines, each driving 16 cells. Suppose that each transistor has a gate capacitance of 0.5 fF and that the Ron of the decoder transistors is 100 Ω. Draw the schematic of the row decoder.

Tarjima: SRAM: 16 ta wordline, har biri 16 ta katakni boshqaradi, CG = 0.5 fF, dekoder tranzistorlari RON = 100 Ω. Qator dekoderi sxemasini chizing.

Javob

Sxema. Javob sxema: 4 ta adres biti A3…A0 va ularning inversiyalari (4 ta inverter) → 16 ta 4 kirishli NAND, har birining chiqishida inverter (drayver) → WL0…WL15. Masalan WL5 (0101) uchun NAND ga Ā3, A2, Ā1, A0 kiradi. (21-dars)

059. Consider a DRAM with 16 wordlines, each driving 16 cells. Assume that each pass-transistor has a gate capacitance of 0.1 fF and that the Ron of the decoder transistors is 40 Ω. Draw the schematic of the row decoder.

Tarjima: DRAM: 16 wordline × 16 katak, CG = 0.1 fF, RON = 40 Ω. Qator dekoderi sxemasini chizing.

Javob

Sxema 002 dagidek. 002 bilan bir xil sxema: 16 wordline uchun 4 bitli dekoder (16 NAND + drayverlar). Raqamlar sxemaga ta'sir qilmaydi, ular 066 da kerak. (21-dars)

066. Consider a DRAM with 16 wordlines, each driving 16 cells. Assume that each pass-transistor has a gate capacitance of 0.1 fF and that the Ron of the decoder transistors is 40 Ω. Calculate the maximum cell activation delay (only the delay of the last stage of the decoder).

Tarjima: Xuddi shu DRAM: katakni yoqishning maksimal kechikishini hisoblang (faqat dekoderning oxirgi bosqichi).

Javob

≈ 44 fs. DRAM katagida wordline ga 1 ta kalit: C = 16 · 0.1 fF = 1.6 fF. t = 0.69 · 40 · 1.6·10⁻¹⁵ = 44·10⁻¹⁵ s. (21-dars)

102. Consider an SRAM with 16 wordlines, each driving 16 cells. Suppose that each transistor has a gate capacity of 0.5 fF and that the Ron of the decoder transistors is 100 Ω. Calculate the maximum cell activation delay (only the delay of the last stage of the decoder): ……

Tarjima: SRAM (16 × 16, CG = 0.5 fF, RON = 100 Ω): oxirgi bosqich kechikishi?

Javob

≈ 1.1 ps. SRAM katagida wordline 2 ta kalitni boshqaradi: C = 16 · 2 · 0.5 fF = 16 fF. t = 0.69 · 100 · 16·10⁻¹⁵ = 1.1·10⁻¹² s. Tuzoq: "2" ni unutish (0.55 ps chiqadi). (21-dars)

3. FPGA va LUT

k kirishli LUT: 2k katak, 22k funksiya Funksiyada k dan ko'p kirish bo'lsa, uni bo'laklarga ajrating: har bo'lak ≤ k kirish.

067. An FPGA uses only 4-input Look-up-Table (LUT) to perform combinatorial logic functions. To achieve O = A B C (D + E + F) are needed:

  1. 5 LUT
  2. 3 LUT
  3. 2 LUT
  4. functions with 6 inputs cannot be implemented

Tarjima: FPGA faqat 4 kirishli LUT ishlatadi. O = A·B·C·(D + E + F) uchun nechta LUT kerak?

Javob

(c) 2 LUT. 1-LUT: X = D + E + F (3 kirish). 2-LUT: O = A·B·C·X (4 kirish). Jami 2 ta. 6 kirishli funksiya ham bo'laklab bajariladi, shuning uchun (d) noto'g'ri. (10-dars)
