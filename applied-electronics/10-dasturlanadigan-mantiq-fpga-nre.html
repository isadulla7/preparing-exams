10. Dasturlanadigan mantiq: PAL, PLA, FPGA, LUT va NRE xarajati

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: mantiqiy sxemani "zavodda qotirmasdan", foydalanuvchi o'zi dasturlab yaratadigan mikrosxemalarni (PAL, PLA, CPLD, FPGA), FPGA ichidagi asosiy g'isht bo'lgan LUT ni va mahsulot narxini hisoblashni (NRE va dona narxi, kesishish nuqtasi). Imtihonda: 2021-01 imtihonidagi 7 ballik NRE masalasi, 2021-06 va 2021-07 testlari (LUT), 005, 067, 084, 112-savollar. Manba: B6 slaydlari (Programmable Logic). O'qish vaqti: 35 daqiqa.
O'xshatish: kiyim. Full custom = tikuvchiga buyurtma: aynan sizga mos, lekin qimmat va uzoq. Semi-custom = tayyor qolipdan tikish. Dasturlanadigan mantiq = do'kondan sotib olingan, kamari va yenglari sozlanadigan kiyim: darhol kiyasiz, lekin har bir dona biroz qimmatroq va "ideal" emas.

2. AND-OR tekisliklari: PAL, PLA, PROM

Har qanday mantiqiy funksiyani ko'paytmalar yig'indisi (sum of products) ko'rinishida yozish mumkin: avval bir nechta AND (ko'paytma), keyin ularni OR (yig'indi). Masalan O = I1·I4' + I2'·I3 (' = inkor, NOT). Shuning uchun dasturlanadigan chip ichida ikki "tekislik" bor: AND tekisligi va OR tekisligi.

Chizmada vertikal simlar = kirishlar va ularning inkorlari, gorizontal chiziq = bitta AND darvozasi. Kesishmadagi X = "shu kirish shu AND ga ulangan". Dasturlash = qaysi kesishmaga X qo'yishni tanlash.

Qaysi tekislik dasturlanishiga qarab uch xil tuzilma bor:

Chalkashtirmang: PAL = AND dasturlanadi (PAL da "A" bor → AND). PLA = ikkalasi ham. PROM = faqat OR (AND = dekoder).

3. SPLD → CPLD → FPGA

  • SPLD (Simple PLD): bitta AND-OR tuzilma + chiqish mantiqi. Chiqishda tri-state bufer (OE = 1 → chiqish, OE = 0 → oyoqcha kirish bo'lib ishlaydi) va flip-flop (registr) bor. Registr chiqishi AND tekisligiga qaytib keladi (feedback), shuning uchun ketma-ket (sequential) sxema, masalan hisoblagich yasash mumkin (7–9-darslar).
  • CPLD (Complex PLD): bitta chipda bir nechta SPLD + ular orasidagi dasturlanadigan ulanishlar. Ikki darajada dasturlanadi: (1) har bir PLD bloki, (2) bloklar orasidagi ulanishlar.
  • FPGA (Field Programmable Gate Array, "joyida dasturlanadigan darvozalar massivi"): millionlab ekvivalent darvoza. Uch xil dasturlanadigan qismdan iborat:

Misol: Xilinx XC4000 ning CLB bloki (slayddagi): ichida 3 ta LUT bor: F va G (har biri 4 kirishli) hamda H (3 kirishli; uning ikki kirishi F va G chiqishidan kelishi mumkin). Yana 2 ta flip-flop (umumiy clock, clock enable, set/reset). Ya'ni CLB = kombinatsion mantiq (LUT) + xotira (flip-flop).

Zamonaviy FPGA larda tayyor qo'shimcha bloklar ham bor: qo'shuvchi va ko'paytiruvchi (DSP), xotira (block RAM), hatto butun protsessor.

4. LUT: funksiyani xotiraga yozish

LUT (Look-Up Table, "qarab topish jadvali") = kichik xotira + multipleksor. Funksiyani formula bilan hisoblamaydi: haqiqat jadvali (truth table) ni oldindan yozib qo'yadi. Kirishlar adres bo'lib xizmat qiladi va kerakli katakchani tanlaydi.

Hisob:

  • k kirish → 2k ta kombinatsiya → 2k ta 1 bitli xotira katakchasi.
  • Har katakchaga 0 yoki 1 yoziladi → katakchalarni to'ldirish usullari soni = 2(2k) = shuncha xil funksiya.

Imtihon savollari

2021-06 (test). A three-input look-up table (LUT) in the logic blocks of an FPGA: (a) Can implement up to 8 logic functions with three inputs. (b) Can implement up to 2^(2^3) logic functions with three inputs. (c) Has 2^(2^3) memory cells. (d) Can implement up to 2^(2^4) logic functions with four inputs.

YechimRasmiy yechim: 3 kirish 8 ta katakchani tanlaydi, har katakcha 2 xil qiymat (0, 1) saqlaydi → 28 = 2(23) = 256 funksiya. (a) xato: 8 = katakchalar soni, funksiyalar emas. (c) xato: katakchalar 23 = 8 ta. (d) xato: 3 kirishli LUT 4 kirishli funksiya qila olmaydi. Javob: (b).

2021-07 (test). A Look-Up Table with 4 inputs: (a) Has 4 memory cells. (b) Uses 2^4 1-bit memory cells (c) Has 512 memory cells. (d) Has 2^8 memory cells.

Yechim4 kirish → 24 = 16 kombinatsiya → 16 ta 1 bitli katakcha. Javob: (b).

067. An FPGA uses only 4-input Look-up-Table (LUT) to perform combinatorial logic functions. To achieve O = A B C (D + E + F) are needed: a) 5 LUT   b) 3 LUT   c) 2 LUT   d) functions with 6 inputs cannot be implemented

YechimFunksiyada 6 ta kirish bor (A…F), bitta 4 kirishli LUT ga sig'maydi. Funksiyani ikki bo'lakka bo'lamiz: avval X = D + E + F (3 kirish → 1-LUT), keyin O = A·B·C·X (4 kirish → 2-LUT). Javob: (c) 2 LUT. (d) xato: LUT larni ketma-ket ulab istalgan katta funksiyani qurish mumkin.
Qoida: har bir 4 kirishli LUT "signallar sonini" 4 tadan 1 taga kamaytiradi, ya'ni 3 taga. n kirishli AND/OR turidagi funksiya uchun kerakli LUT soni ≈ ⌈(n − 1)/3⌉. 6 kirish: ⌈5/3⌉ = 2.

6. Narx: NRE va dona narxi

Mahsulot narxi ikki qismdan iborat:

  • NRE (Non-Recurring Engineering) = bir martalik xarajat: loyihalash, maskalar, uskunalar. Nechta dona yasashingizdan qat'i nazar bir marta to'lanadi.
  • Dona narxi (unit cost) = bitta donani ishlab chiqarish narxi (material + ish haqi, loyihalash kirmaydi).
Jami narx = NRE + dona narxi · N Bir mahsulot narxi = Jami / N = NRE / N + dona narxi
O'xshatish: to'y oshi. Qozon, o'choq, oshpaz haqi (NRE) bir marta to'lanadi. Har bir mehmonga ketadigan guruch va go'sht (dona narxi) mehmon soniga ko'paytiriladi. Mehmon qancha ko'p bo'lsa, bir kishiga to'g'ri keladigan qozon xarajati shuncha kichik.

Slayddagi misol (uchta texnologiya):

NREDona narxiXarakteri
A$2 000$100kam NRE, qimmat dona (masalan protsessor)
B$30 000$30o'rtacha (masalan FPGA)
C$100 000$2katta NRE, arzon dona (masalan ASIC)

Kesishish nuqtasi (break-even, NBE) = ikki usulning jami narxi teng bo'lgan dona soni:

NRE₁ + u₁·N = NRE₂ + u₂·N  →  NBE = (NRE₂ − NRE₁) / (u₁ − u₂)

A va B: (30 000 − 2 000)/(100 − 30) = 400 dona. B va C: (100 000 − 30 000)/(30 − 2) = 2 500 dona. Demak: 400 dan kam → A, 400–2 500 → B, 2 500 dan ko'p → C arzon.

Bozorga chiqish vaqti

Narxdan tashqari vaqt ham muhim. Daromad uchburchak shaklida: bozor o'sadi, keyin mahsulot eskiradi. Kechikib chiqqan mahsulot kichikroq uchburchakni oladi: slaydga ko'ra kechikish daromadni kvadratik kamaytiradi. Shuning uchun boshqalar yasagan tayyor komponentlar (protsessor, FPGA) ishlatiladi: dizayn vaqti va NRE kamayadi.

Imtihon savollari

005. A SW implementation of a programmable processor application has: a) lower unit cost than a dedicated HW   b) higher performance than a dedicated HW   c) lower NRE cost than a dedicated HW   d) lower flexibility than a dedicated HW

YechimProtsessor tayyor, faqat dastur yoziladi → loyihalash arzon (kichik NRE). Lekin protsessor qimmat (dona narxi yuqori), sekinroq, ammo moslashuvchanroq. Javob: (c).

112. A dedicated hardware implementation of an application has: a) lower unit cost than a software using a CPU   b) lower performance than a software using a CPU   c) lower NRE cost than a software using a CPU   d) greater flexibility than a software using a CPU

Yechim005 ning teskarisi: maxsus apparat (ASIC) = katta NRE, lekin arzon dona, yuqori tezlik, past moslashuvchanlik. Javob: (a).

084. If the non-recurring costs NRE to manufacture an integrated circuit double, in order to keep the cost per product constant, it is generally necessary to: a) halve the engineers' wages   b) double the selling price of each chip   c) double the number of chips sold   d) halve the unit cost of the chip

YechimBir mahsulot narxi = NRE/N + dona narxi. NRE 2 baravar oshsa, NRE/N o'zgarmasligi uchun N ham 2 baravar oshishi kerak: 2·NRE / 2N = NRE/N. (b) sotish narxi — bu xarajat emas. Javob: (c).

7. Imtihon masalasi: 2021-01, Question 2 (7 ball)

To ramp up the production and sales of a new product, a company has three major options: using microprocessors with an NRE cost of 40000 USD and a unit cost of 25 USD; using FPGAs with an NRE cost of 110000 USD and a unit cost of 15 USD; using an ASIC with an NRE cost of 950000 USD and a unit cost of 2 USD. Considering these data, calculate:
1. The break-even number of units NBE, for which the total cost for their production using FPGAs would be the same as their production using microprocessors (2 points)
2. Compared to the above, how is the break-even number of units between the production using ASICs and the production using microprocessors (higher / lower / unchanged) (2 points)
3. The total cost if are produced 40000 units using FPGAs and 400000 units using ASICs (3 points)

1-qadam (NBE, µP va FPGA). Ikkala jami narxni tenglaymiz:

40 000 + 25·N = 110 000 + 15·N  →  10·N = 70 000  →  NBE = 7 000

Tekshiruv: µP: 40 000 + 25·7 000 = 215 000; FPGA: 110 000 + 15·7 000 = 215 000 ✓.

2-qadam (ASIC va µP).

40 000 + 25·N = 950 000 + 2·N  →  23·N = 910 000  →  N ≈ 39 565

39 565 > 7 000 → higher. Mantiqan: ASIC ning NRE si juda katta, uni "qoplash" uchun ko'p dona kerak.

3-qadam (jami narx). Ikki alohida partiya, har biriga o'z NRE si:

FPGA: 110 000 + 15 · 40 000 = 710 000 USD ASIC: 950 000 + 2 · 400 000 = 1 750 000 USD Jami = 710 000 + 1 750 000 = 2 460 000 USD
Rasmiy javoblar: 1) 7000   2) higher   3) 2460000.
Tez-tez xato: 3-qadamda NRE ni unutish yoki uni dona soniga ko'paytirish. NRE har bir texnologiya uchun bir marta qo'shiladi.

Qo'shimcha (savolda yo'q, lekin foydali): FPGA va ASIC kesishishi (950 000 − 110 000)/(15 − 2) ≈ 64 615 dona. Demak: 7 000 gacha µP, 7 000 – 64 615 FPGA, undan ko'p ASIC eng arzon.

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

k kirishli LUT: 2k katakcha, 2(2k) funksiya (3 → 8 va 256; 4 → 16 va 65 536) Jami = NRE + u·N  ·  Bir dona = NRE/N + u  ·  NBE = ΔNRE / Δu
  • PAL: AND dasturlanadi, OR qotirilgan. PLA: ikkalasi. PROM: AND qotirilgan (dekoder), OR dasturlanadi.
  • CPLD = bir nechta SPLD + dasturlanadigan ulanishlar. FPGA = CLB (LUT + flip-flop) + ulanishlar (switch matrix) + IOB.
  • Katta funksiyani LUT larga bo'lib qurish: har 4 kirishli LUT signallar sonini 3 taga kamaytiradi (067 → 2 LUT).
  • SW (protsessor): kichik NRE, qimmat dona, sekin, moslashuvchan. Maxsus HW (ASIC): katta NRE, arzon dona, tez, moslashmaydi.
  • NRE 2 baravar → bir dona narxi o'zgarmasligi uchun sotuvni 2 baravar oshirish kerak.
  • Konfiguratsiya: SRAM (uchuvchan), EPROM/EEPROM/Flash, fuse va antifuse (OTP).
  • Javoblar: 005 c; 112 a; 084 c; 067 c; 2021-06 LUT (b); 2021-07 LUT (b); 2021-01: 7000, higher, 2 460 000 USD.
