20. Mashq: A/D va D/A (20 ta savol bir joyda)

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: yangi nazariya yo'q. Bazadagi 20 ta A/D–D/A savolining hammasi shu yerda, javoblari va qaysi darsda tushuntirilgani bilan. Imtihonda savollar bitta "ssenariy" atrofida guruh bo'lib keladi (bir tizim haqida 3–4 savol), shuning uchun ularni ssenariy bo'yicha qayta yechamiz. Avval o'zingiz yeching, keyin "Yechim" ni oching. O'qish vaqti: 40 daqiqa.

Ssenariy A: 4 kanal, ±1 V, Fmax = 2 kHz, ADC 0–10 V

A group of four analog signals has (for each channel) levels between –1 V and +1 V, maximum frequency Fmax = 2 kHz. The A/D converter has input dynamic from 0 V to +10 V. The overall sampling frequency Fs is four times the minimum.
  1. 060 / 071: blok sxemani chizing.
  2. 093: kuchaytirgichning gain va input offseti.
  3. 109: bipolyar kvantlash xatosi 0.1% dan kichik bo'lishi uchun minimal bitlar.
  4. Qo'shimcha: umumiy Fs qancha?
Yechim
  1. Himoya → kuchaytirgich → LPF (har kanalda) → MUX → S/H → ADC.
  2. G = 10/2 = 5; input offset +1 V.
  3. 1/2N+1 < 0.001 → 9 bit.
  4. Minimum 2 · 2 = 4 kHz, to'rt baravari 16 kHz kanalga, 4 kanal → 64 kHz.

Ssenariy B: 4 kanal, Fmax lar har xil, tracking ADC

A group of four analog signals has (for each channel) levels from –1V to +1V, maximum frequency of the individual channels Fmax,1 = 3 kHz, Fmax,2 = 2.5 kHz, Fmax,3 = 1 kHz, Fmax,4 = 2 kHz. The A/D converter is a tracking circuit with input dynamics from 0 V to +10 V.
  1. 035: tracking ADC blok sxemasi; Tconv,max = 8.5 μs, fck = 125 MHz da maksimal bitlar.
  2. 051: kuchaytirgich rezistorlari (VREF = +10 V).
Yechim
  1. Komparator → U/D hisoblagich → DAC → komparatorga qayta. 125 MHz · 8.5 μs = 1062 takt, 2N ≤ 1062 → 10 bit.
  2. R4/R3 = 1/10; V+ = 1.82 V da Vout = 10 V → R2/R1 = 4.5.

Diqqat: kanallar Fmax i har xil bo'lsa, umumiy MUX bitta tezlikda aylansa, hamma kanal eng tez kanal (3 kHz) bo'yicha hisoblanadi.

Ssenariy C: 4 kanal, 8 bitli tracking ADC, 100 MHz

A four-channel acquisition system uses an eight-bit tracking A/D converter with 100 MHz clock frequency and a Sample & Hold circuit with 1 μs acquisition time. For each channel, we want to have a sampling frequency Fs equal to three times the maximum frequency Fmax of the signal and an eight-pole anti-aliasing filter.
  1. 013: har kanal uchun Fmax.
  2. 053: SNRq, SNRa, SNRtot (sinus).
  3. 071: blok sxema.
Yechim
  1. Tconv = 256/100 MHz = 2.56 μs; Fconv = 1/3.56 μs ≈ 280 kHz; kanalga 70 kHz; Fmax = 70/3 ≈ 23.3 kHz.
  2. SNRq ≈ 50 dB; (70 − 23)/23 ≈ 2 → SNRa = 8 · 6 · 1 = 48 dB; SNRtot ≈ 43 dB (rasmiy, amplitudalar yig'indisi).
  3. Himoya → kuchaytirgich → LPF (8 qutb) → MUX → S/H → tracking ADC.

Ssenariy D: N kanal, Fmax = 5 kHz, Tacq 4 μs, Tconv 6 μs

Consider N analog signals with a maximum frequency of 5 kHz. The signals are converted into a digital with a S/H and an A/D converter which have a 4 μs acquisition time and a 6 μs conversion time, respectively.
  1. 049: kvantlash xatosi 0.2% dan kichik bo'lishi uchun bitlar.
  2. 095: K = 2.5 da maksimal kanallar soni va blok sxema.
Yechim
  1. 1/2N < 0.002 → 9 bit (1/2N+1 bilan 8 ham qabul qilinadi).
  2. Fs,max = 100 kHz; kanalga 2.5 · 2 · 5 = 25 kHz → 4 kanal.

Ssenariy E: 2 kanal, 0–8 MHz, ±1 V, Tacq 4 ns

An A/D conversion system has two input channels. On each channel there is a bipolar signal with effective value 50 mVeff and a frequency band 0→8 MHz. The A/D converter has an input dynamic of ±1 V. We have also a S/H circuit with the acquisition time (Tacq) of 4 ns.
  1. 076: blok sxema; har kanal minimumdan 4 baravar tez bo'lishi uchun Tconv.
  2. 108: 8 bit, komparator 2 ns. Qaysi ADC turi, murakkabligi.
  3. 045: aliasing xatosi = maksimal kvantlash xatosi bo'lishi uchun filtr talablari.
Yechim
  1. Kanalga 4 · 16 = 64 MS/s, jami 128 MS/s → Ts = 7.8 ns → Tconv = 3.8 ns.
  2. 3.8 ns ga faqat bitta 2 ns solishtirish sig'adi → flash, 28 − 1 = 255 komparator.
  3. Kesish 8 MHz; alias 64 − 8 = 56 MHz; 20·lg 256 = 48 dB; lg(56/8) = 0.845 dek → 56.8 dB/dek → 3 qutb.

Qiziq tomoni: signal samarali qiymati 50 mV, ADC esa ±1 V. Kuchaytirgich kerak (sinus cho'qqisi 50·√2 ≈ 71 mV → G ≈ 1/0.071 ≈ 14). Bu savolda so'ralmagan, lekin imtihonda so'ralishi mumkin.

2018-03, Exercise 2 (5 ball)

A triangular signal with 5kHz frequency is digitalized with a 12 bit AD converter, with an input range of 5 V. 1. Find the SNR when the input signal has peak to peak amplitude of 2.5 V (2 points) 2. If the spectrum of the signal may be considered 0 after the 8th harmonic which is the minimum sampling frequency (3 points)

Yechim

(1) Uchburchak: 6N = 72 dB. Signal diapazonning yarmi: 20·lg(5/2.5) = 6 dB. SNR = 72 − 6 = 66 dB.

(2) Garmonika = asosiy chastotaga karrali chastota (5, 10, 15 … kHz). 8-garmonika 8 · 5 = 40 kHz → signal kengligi B = 40 kHz. Nyquist: fs ≥ 2B = 80 kHz.

2019-02, Exercise 1 (14 ball)

A successive approximations 7 bit A/D converter has a clock of 2 MHz and an input range from 0V to 5 V. If at the input there is a constant voltage of 2.6V and a conversion is started compute: 1) The sequence of values at the output of the internal D/A converter as a function of the time (7 points) 2) The number of cycles required for the conversion (1 point) 3) The final digital output voltage (1 point). If the converter is used to sample and convert the values of a sinusoidal waveform with frequency f0, average value of 3V and peak to peak value of 2.5 V which are 4. The maximum frequency f0 of the signal (2 points) 5. The Signal-to-Noise Ratio (SNR) in dB associated to the conversion (3 points)

Yechim

(1) Bitlar: 2.5 ≤ 2.6 → 1; 3.75 → 0; 3.125 → 0; 2.8125 → 0; 2.65625 → 0; 2.578 ≤ 2.6 → 1; 2.617 → 0.

(2) 7 takt (7 · 0.5 µs = 3.5 µs). (3) Kod 1000010 = 66 (66 · 5/128 = 2.578 V).

(4) fc = 2 MHz / 7 = 285.7 kHz (maksimal namuna olish chastotasi) → f0 ≤ fc/2 = 142.9 kHz.

(5) Sinus: 6 · 7 + 1.76 = 43.76 dB; Vpp = 2.5 V diapazon 5 V ning yarmi → −6 dB. SNR = 37.8 dB (rasmiy varaqda 37.6). Rasmiy izoh: "average does not count: it is the peak-to-peak value which counts".

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

Ssenariy masalasini yechish tartibi

  1. Blok sxema: himoya → kuchaytirgich → LPF → MUX → S/H → ADC.
  2. Kanalga Fs: K·2·Fmax (yoki savoldagi qoida). Jami: × kanallar.
  3. Vaqt: Ts = Tacq + Tconv. ADC turi: Tconv ga qarab (flash 1, SAR N, tracking 2N takt).
  4. Bitlar: xato foizidan yoki SNR dan.
  5. Filtr: alias = Fs − Fmax, kerakli dB → qutblar.
  6. Kuchaytirgich: G va offset.

SAR masalasi: DAC 1-taktda FS/2; Vin ≥ DAC → 1, aks holda 0; qadam har safar yarmi. N bit = N takt, Tc = N/fck, f0 ≤ 1/(2Tc). SNR: uchburchak 6N, sinus 6N + 1.76, minus 20·lg(diapazon/Vpp); o'rtacha qiymat ahamiyatsiz.

Ikki baravar teng SNR yig'indisi: quvvatlar qo'shilsa −3 dB, amplitudalar qo'shilsa −6 dB.

O'qib bo'lgach chatga yozing: "tushundim" yoki "tushunmadim" (qaysi joyi qiyinligini ayting).
