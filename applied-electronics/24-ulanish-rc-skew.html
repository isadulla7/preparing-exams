24. Sim nega "bitta nuqta" emas: RC model, uzatish vaqti va skew

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: bir mikrosxema ikkinchisiga signal yuborganda, signal nega kechikib yetishini; bu kechikish qanday hisoblanishini (RC model); uzatish vaqti tTX va skew tK nima ekanini; jitter nima ekanini. Keyingi uchta dars (25, 26, 27) shu tushunchalar ustiga quriladi. 2021-01 imtihonidagi 7 ballik masala to'liq shu darsga tegishli. Manba: C1 slaydlari. O'qish vaqti: 30 daqiqa.

1. Muammo: 0 va 1 aslida kuchlanish

Raqamli sxemada biz "0" va "1" deb gapiramiz. Lekin simda aslida kuchlanish va tok yuradi (5-darsni eslang: 0 = past kuchlanish, 1 = yuqori kuchlanish). Signal bir mikrosxemadan ikkinchisiga sim (plata yo'lakchasi, kabel) orqali boradi. Mana bu yerda ikkita so'z kerak:

  • Driver (uzatuvchi): signalni simga chiqaradigan mantiqiy element chiqishi.
  • Receiver (qabul qilgich): simdan signalni o'qiydigan mantiqiy element kirishi.

Shu ikkalasini bog'laydigan sim interconnection (ulanish) deyiladi.

Hayotiy o'xshatish: quvurdan suv keladi. Jo'mrakni ochsangiz, suv boshqa uchidan darhol emas, biroz keyin chiqadi, avval sekin, keyin to'liq oqim. Elektr signal ham shunday: sim "bo'sh quvur" kabi avval to'lishi kerak.

Ideal ulanish: sim ekvipotensial (butun sim bo'ylab kuchlanish bir xil), ya'ni "bitta tugun". Driver chiqishi = receiver kirishi. Haqiqiy ulanish: simning qarshiligi, sig'imi, induktivligi bor, signal kechikadi va shovqin qo'shiladi. Signalni to'g'ri yetkazish masalalari umumiy nom bilan signal integrity (signal yaxlitligi) deyiladi.

Slayd buni ta'kidlaydi: hozirgi chiplarda milliardlab tranzistor bor, lekin tezlikni ko'pincha tranzistorlar emas, ulanishlar cheklaydi (signal va quvvatni tarqatish).

2. Driver va receiver modeli

Hisoblash uchun mantiqiy elementlarni oddiy elementlar bilan almashtiramiz (1–3-darslardagi R va C):

  • Driver = ideal kuchlanish manbai VA (0 V yoki VDD) + ketma-ket chiqish qarshiligi RO.
  • Receiver = kirish qarshiligi RI (CMOS uchun RI → ∞, ya'ni tok olmaydi) + kirish sig'imi CI.

Eng oddiy model: sim bitta tugun (B ≡ C). Shunda RO va C birgalikda 3-darsdagi RC zanjirni hosil qiladi. C ga receiver sig'imi CI va simning o'z sig'imi kiradi.

3. RC zanjirda kuchlanish qanday o'sadi (3-darsni eslaymiz)

Driver 0 dan VDD ga sakraganda kondensator darhol zaryadlanmaydi. Uning kuchlanishi eksponensial o'sadi:

L→H (pastdan yuqoriga): VC(t) = VDD · (1 − e−t/τ)     τ = RO · C H→L (yuqoridan pastga): VC(t) = VDD · e−t/τ

τ (tau) = vaqt doimiysi. t = τ bo'lganda kuchlanish yakuniy qiymatning 63% iga yetadi (1 − e−1 = 0.632).

Formuladan vaqtni topish (bu imtihonda doim kerak). VC(t) = V bo'ladigan vaqt:

L→H: t = τ · ln( VDD / (VDD − V) )     H→L: t = τ · ln( VDD / V )
ln nima? ln = natural logarifm, ex ning teskarisi: agar ex = a bo'lsa, x = ln(a). Kalkulyatorda "ln" tugmasi. Misol: ln(2.5) = 0.916, ln(1.25) = 0.223.

4. Qabul qilgich qachon "1" ni sezadi: VIL, VIH va VT

5-darsdan: qabul qilgich kirishi uchun ikkita chegara bor.

  • VIL: kirish shundan past bo'lsa, aniq "0".
  • VIH: kirish shundan yuqori bo'lsa, aniq "1".
  • Ular orasida haqiqiy o'tish chegarasi VT (threshold) bor. VT aniq qayerda ekani noma'lum: u VIL va VIH orasida istalgan joyda bo'lishi mumkin (har bir mikrosxemada har xil, haroratga qarab ham o'zgaradi).

Shuning uchun biz eng yomon ikki holatni olamiz:

  • VT eng past (= VIL) bo'lsa, qabul qilgich o'zgarishni eng erta sezadi → tTXmin.
  • VT eng yuqori (= VIH) bo'lsa, eng kech sezadi → tTXmax.

5. Uzatish vaqti tTX va skew tK

Uzatish vaqti (transmission time) tTX: driver chiqishi o'zgargan lahzadan qabul qilgich yangi mantiqiy holatni sezgan lahzagacha bo'lgan vaqt. U quyidagilarga bog'liq: driver sathlari (VH, VL), qabul qilgich chegarasi VT, RO, C, ish sharoiti (VDD, harorat), qabul qilgichning simdagi joyi.

Bu parametrlar bir xil emas (tarqoq), shuning uchun tTX ham aniq son emas, balki oraliq: tTXmin dan tTXmax gacha.

Skew: tK = tTXmax − tTXmin

Skew (inglizcha "qiyshayish, nomuvofiqlik") = signal qachon yetib kelishidagi noaniqlik. Biz faqat "tTXmin dan tTXmax gacha bo'lgan oraliqda biror paytda" deya olamiz.

Skew va jitter farqi

  • Skew tK: ikkita signal (yoki bitta signalning ikki joydagi nusxasi) bir vaqtdagi o'tishlari orasidagi farq.
  • Jitter tJ: bitta signal (odatda takt) davrining davrdan davrga o'zgarishi.

Ikkalasi ham vaqt zaxiralarini kamaytiradi. 8-darsdagi Fmax formulasiga jitter qo'shiladi:

TCKmin = TCO + TL + TSU + TJ     Fmax = 1 / TCKmin

Ulanishlar ham kechikish qo'shadi, shuning uchun haqiqiy sxemada TCKmin ga simlardagi kechikishlar ham qo'shiladi.

6. Slayddagi mashq C1.1 (to'liq yechim)

Shart: CMOS driver va receiver ekvipotensial sim bilan ulangan. Driver: VDD = 5 V, RO = 120 Ω. Receiver: RI → ∞, VIH = 3 V, VIL = 1 V. Umumiy sig'im C = 80 pF. L→H o'tish uchun tTX va tK ni toping.

1-qadam. τ: τ = RO · C = 120 Ω · 80 pF = 9600 · 10−12 s = 9.6 ns.

2-qadam. tTXmin (VC = VIL = 1 V bo'lgan payt):
t = τ · ln(5 / (5 − 1)) = 9.6 · ln(1.25) = 9.6 · 0.223 = 2.14 ns.

3-qadam. tTXmax (VC = VIH = 3 V bo'lgan payt):
t = τ · ln(5 / (5 − 3)) = 9.6 · ln(2.5) = 9.6 · 0.916 = 8.80 ns.

4-qadam. Skew: tK = 8.80 − 2.14 = 6.66 ns.

Slayddagi savol: "VDD o'zgarsa, tTX o'zgaradimi?" Birinchi yaqinlashishda yo'q, agar chegaralar VDD ga proporsional bo'lsa (CMOS da VT ≈ VDD/2). Formulada faqat VDD/(VDD − V) nisbati qatnashadi; VDD va V birga o'zgarsa, nisbat o'zgarmaydi.

7. Imtihon masalasi: 2021-01 (7 ball)

"A rail-to-rail driver supplied between Vgnd = 0 V and Vdd = 3.3 V and an output resistance R0 = 250 Ω drives through an equipotential transmission line a receiver with input capacitance Ci = 8 pF and negligible resistance. Assuming that: all low-to-high transitions start with the transmission line settled to Vgnd; all high-to-low transitions start with the transmission line settled to Vdd; calculate for a maximum transmission time TTX = 2 ns: the maximum receiver threshold for the high level VIH = ? V (2 points); the minimum receiver threshold for the low level VIL = ? V (2 points). If the driver output resistance R0 increases and we want to maintain the same transmission time TTX = 2 ns, then the receiver noise margins must (3 points): decrease in the same proportion as R0 / increase in the same proportion as R0 / decrease, but not in the same proportion as R0 / increase, but not in the same proportion as R0"

Tarjima: 3.3 V li driver (RO = 250 Ω) ekvipotensial sim orqali Ci = 8 pF li qabul qilgichni boshqaradi. Uzatish vaqti ko'pi bilan 2 ns bo'lishi uchun VIH eng ko'pi bilan qancha, VIL eng kamida qancha bo'lishi kerak? RO oshsa, shovqin zaxiralari qanday o'zgarishi kerak?

Fikr: 2 ns ichida qabul qilgich o'zgarishni aniq sezishi kerak. L→H da: 2 ns dan keyin VC hali VIH ga yetmagan bo'lsa, kechikamiz. Demak VIH ≤ VC(2 ns). H→L da: VIL ≥ VC(2 ns).

Javob (yechim)

τ = 250 Ω · 8 pF = 2 ns. t = 2 ns = τ, demak e−t/τ = e−1 = 0.368.

VIH ≤ 3.3 · (1 − 0.368) = 3.3 · 0.632 = 2.09 V.

VIL ≥ 3.3 · 0.368 = 1.21 V.

RO oshsa, τ oshadi, kuchlanish sekinroq o'zgaradi. 2 ns da VC(L→H) pastroq, demak VIH pastroq bo'lishi kerak; VC(H→L) yuqoriroq, demak VIL yuqoriroq bo'lishi kerak. Ikkalasi o'rtaga yaqinlashadi.

Shovqin zaxiralari: NMH = VOH − VIH (VIH tushdi → NMH oshdi), NML = VIL − VOL (VIL ko'tarildi → NML oshdi). Bog'liqlik eksponensial, chiziqli emas. Javob: "increase, but not in the same proportion as R0".

O'zingizni tekshiring:
  1. RO = 100 Ω, C = 50 pF, VDD = 5 V, VIL = 1.5 V, VIH = 3.5 V. L→H uchun tTXmin, tTXmax, tK?
  2. Skew va jitter farqi nimada?
  3. C ikki baravar oshsa, tTX qanday o'zgaradi?
Javoblar
  1. τ = 5 ns. tTXmin (VIL da) = 5 · ln(5/(5 − 1.5)) = 5 · ln(1.43) = 5 · 0.357 = 1.78 ns. tTXmax (VIH da) = 5 · ln(5/(5 − 3.5)) = 5 · ln(3.33) = 5 · 1.204 = 6.02 ns. tK = 4.24 ns.
  2. Skew: ikki signal orasidagi (yoki ikki joydagi) farq. Jitter: bitta signal davrining o'zgarishi.
  3. τ = RC ikki baravar, demak tTX ham ikki baravar (tTX ∝ C).

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

τ = RO · C  ·  L→H: VC = VDD(1 − e−t/τ)  ·  H→L: VC = VDD·e−t/τ L→H: t = τ·ln(VDD/(VDD − V))  ·  H→L: t = τ·ln(VDD/V) L→H: tTXmin → VIL da, tTXmax → VIH da  ·  tK = tTXmax − tTXmin TCKmin = TCO + TL + TSU + TJ (+ ulanish kechikishlari)
  • Haqiqiy sim bitta nuqta emas: kechikish va shovqin bor (signal integrity).
  • Driver = VA + RO; CMOS receiver = RI → ∞ va CI.
  • tTX bog'liq: VH/VL, VT, RO, C, VDD, harorat, joy. tTX ∝ C va ∝ RO.
  • Skew = ikki signal farqi; jitter = bitta signal davri o'zgarishi.
  • Javoblar: C1.1 → τ = 9.6 ns, 2.14 / 8.80 ns, tK = 6.66 ns; 2021-01 → VIH ≤ 2.09 V, VIL ≥ 1.21 V, zaxiralar "increase, but not in the same proportion".
