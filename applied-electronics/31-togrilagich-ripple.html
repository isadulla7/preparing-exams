31. Ta'minot bloki: to'g'rilagich, filtr kondensatori va ripple

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: rozetkadagi o'zgaruvchan (ac) kuchlanishdan elektronika uchun doimiy (dc) kuchlanish qanday olinishini: transformator, to'g'rilagich (diodlar) va filtr kondensatori. Asosiy formula: ripple VR = IO/(f·C) (bir yarim davrli) va IO/(2f·C) (ikki yarim davrli). Bu B mavzu: 2021-07 imtihon testi, 070 va 094-savollar, keyingi darsdagi 2020-09 masalasining birinchi yarmi shu yerda. Manba: E1, E2 slaydlari va Ee2 mashqi. O'qish vaqti: 30 daqiqa.

1. Nega ac → dc kerak

Elektr tarmog'i ac (alternating current, o'zgaruvchan: sinus, 220 V, 50 Hz) beradi: uni uzoqqa uzatish va transformator bilan kuchlanishini o'zgartirish oson. Elektron sxemalar (mikrosxemalar, protsessor) esa dc (direct current, doimiy kuchlanish, masalan 5 V) da ishlaydi. Demak o'zgartirish kerak. "Klassik" ta'minot bloki (power supply unit, PSU) besh qismdan iborat:

  • Transformator: 220 V ni kerakli kichikroq ac ga aylantiradi va galvanik izolyatsiya beradi (chiqish tarmoqqa sim bilan ulanmagan → tok urmaydi). Katta, og'ir, qimmat.
  • To'g'rilagich (rectifier): diodlar. 4-darsdan: diod tokni faqat bir tomonga o'tkazadi. Natija: bir yo'nalishli, lekin hali "to'lqinli" kuchlanish.
  • Filtr kondensatori C (hum filter): to'lqinlarni tekislaydi. Natija: deyarli dc, lekin kichik "tebranish" qoladi: ripple.
  • Stabilizator (regulator): ripple ni va kirish o'zgarishlarini yo'qotib, aniq VO beradi (32-dars).

2. Bir yarim davrli va ikki yarim davrli to'g'rilagich

  • Bir yarim davrli (half-wave): bitta diod. Sinusning faqat musbat yarmi o'tadi, manfiy yarmi "yo'qoladi".
  • Ikki yarim davrli (full-wave, ko'pincha 4 diodli ko'prik): manfiy yarmini ham "ag'darib" musbat qiladi. Natijada har davrda ikkita "do'nglik".
O'xshatish: kondensator = suv baki, diod = vaqti-vaqti bilan ochiladigan quvur, yuklama = pastdagi doim ochiq jo'mrak. Quvur faqat qisqa vaqt (cho'qqida) bakni to'ldiradi, qolgan vaqtda jo'mrak bakni bo'shatadi va sath pasayadi. Bak qancha katta (C katta) yoki jo'mrak qancha kichik (IO kichik) bo'lsa, sath shuncha kam tushadi. To'ldirish tez-tez bo'lsa (ikki yarim davrli, yoki f katta), sath ham kam tushadi.

3. Ripple formulasi

Slayddagi soddalashtirish: kondensator o'zgarmas tok IO bilan chiziqli bo'shaydi va zaryadlar orasidagi vaqt butun davr (yoki yarim davr) deb olinadi. Kondensator formulasi (3-dars): ΔV = I·Δt / C.

Bir yarim davrli: VR = IO · T / C = IO / (f · C) Ikki yarim davrli: VR = IO · (T/2) / C = IO / (2f · C) VDC = Vpeak − VR/2   (diod tushishlari bilan: Vpeak − 2Vd − VR/2 ko'prikda)

Bu yerda Vpeak = Veff · √2 (masalan 220 V eff → 311 V cho'qqi). Veff (RMS, effektiv qiymat) = tarmoq "nomi" (220 V), cho'qqi esa √2 marta katta.

Yana slayddan: diod faqat qisqa vaqt ochiq bo'lgani uchun u orqali katta impuls tok o'tadi (o'rtacha tokdan ~5 marta, agar diod 20% vaqt o'tkazsa). Birinchi yoqishda C bo'sh: juda katta "inrush" tok. Kamchiliklari: og'ir transformator, katta C, impuls toklar shovqin (EMI) chiqaradi. Shuning uchun 10 W dan katta quvvatda Yevropa qoidalari impulsli (switching) manbalarni talab qiladi (33-dars).

4. Imtihon savollari

070. The ripple voltage of a half-wave rectifier is halved if: a) the capacity doubles   b) the frequency is halved   c) the current doubles   d) the input voltage is halved

Javob

a) the capacity doubles. VR = IO/(f·C): C ikki marta → VR yarim. (b va c esa VR ni ikki marta oshiradi, d ta'sir qilmaydi.)

094. In a full-wave rectifier, compared to a half-wave rectifier: a) the ripple output voltage doubles   b) the DC output voltage halves   c) the DC output voltage doubles   d) the ripple output voltage halves

Javob

d) the ripple output voltage halves. Kondensator ikki marta tez-tez zaryadlanadi: T o'rniga T/2. DC qiymat deyarli bir xil (≈ Vpeak).

2021-07 (imtihon). A half-wave rectifier supplied with 220 V and 60 Hz has an output capacitor of 1000 µF. What is the maximum current consumed by the load IL = ___ mA that ensures the ripple on the output voltage is at most 3%? How does the ripple amplitude change if the load current remains unchanged but the input voltage increases by 50%? (decreases by 50% / doubles / unchanged / increases by 50%)

Yechim
  1. Cho'qqi: Vpeak = 220 · √2 = 311 V.
  2. Ruxsat etilgan ripple: VR ≤ 3% · 311 = 9.33 V.
  3. Bir yarim davrli: VR = IL/(f·C) → IL = VR · f · C = 9.33 · 60 · 1000·10−6 = 0.56 A = 560 mA.
  4. Kirish 50% oshdi, IL o'zgarmadi → VR = IL/(f·C) o'zgarmaydi: unchanged.

Rasmiy yechim: IL = 1000·10−3 F · 220 V · √2 · 3% · 60 Hz = 560 mA; "the ripple amplitude does not depend on the input voltage amplitude, provided that the load current remains the same".

5. Slayddagi mashq Ee2 (to'liq yechim)

The circuit in the figure is a half-wave rectifier with filter. VS = 18 Veff at 60 Hz, C = 470 μF. a. Determine the output ripple voltage, VOR, for a load current IO = 90 mA. Under these conditions, calculate the output voltage direct component, VOdc. b. Calculate the output voltage variations (ΔVOdc) and the max ripple (VORmax) for load current IO varying from 0 mA to 100 mA. c. Modify the circuit to use a full-wave rectifier. Calculate the new VOdc and VOR.

Yechim (slaydda yechim yo'q, men hisobladim; diodni ideal deb oldim)

Vpeak = 18 · √2 = 25.46 V.

a) VOR = 0.09 / (60 · 470·10−6) = 3.19 V. VOdc = 25.46 − 3.19/2 = 23.86 V.

b) IO = 0: VOR = 0, VOdc = 25.46 V. IO = 100 mA: VOR = 0.1/(60 · 470·10−6) = 3.55 V (bu VORmax), VOdc = 25.46 − 1.77 = 23.69 V. ΔVOdc = 25.46 − 23.69 = 1.77 V. Ya'ni yuklama o'zgarsa, chiqish ham o'zgaradi: stabilizator kerak.

c) Ikki yarim davrli, IO = 90 mA: VOR = 0.09/(2 · 60 · 470·10−6) = 1.60 V; VOdc = 25.46 − 0.80 = 24.66 V. (Diod tushishi hisobga olinsa: ko'prikda 2 · ≈0.7–1 V kam.)

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

Vpeak = Veff · √2  ·  half-wave: VR = IO/(f·C)  ·  full-wave: VR = IO/(2f·C) VDC = Vpeak − VR/2  ·  ΔV = I·Δt/C
  • PSU: transformator (V ↓, izolyatsiya) → to'g'rilagich → C filtr → stabilizator.
  • Ripple: C ↑ yoki f ↑ → kamayadi; IO ↑ → oshadi; kirish amplitudasiga bog'liq emas (IO bir xil bo'lsa).
  • Full-wave ripple half-wave dagidan 2 marta kichik, DC deyarli bir xil.
  • Diod toki impulsli (inrush, EMI) → katta quvvatda switching manba.
  • Javoblar: 070 a; 094 d; 2021-07: 560 mA, unchanged; Ee2: 3.19 V, 23.86 V; 3.55 V, 1.77 V; 1.60 V, 24.66 V.
