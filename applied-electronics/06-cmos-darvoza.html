06. CMOS mantiqiy darvoza: sathlar, shovqin zaxirasi, quvvat va kechikish

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: mantiqiy darvoza (5-darsdagi NOT, NAND …) ichida aslida nima borligini: ikkita tranzistor-kalit. Undan keyin darvozaning to'rtta "pasport" xususiyati: kuchlanish sathlari (VOH, VIH …), shovqin zaxirasi, quvvat sarfi PD = f·C·V² va kechikish tP ∝ R·C. Bu B mavzu: imtihonlarda har safar 1–2 ta test (040, 041, 074, 107, 081, 090; 2021-06 va 2021-07 testlari) va 2021-06 dagi 7 ballik masala shu yerdan. Manba: B1 slaydlari. O'qish vaqti: 35 daqiqa.

1. CMOS invertor: ikki kalit, biri yuqorida, biri pastda

4-darsda tranzistor = elektr bilan boshqariladigan kalit ekanini ko'rdik. CMOS (complementary MOS) texnologiyasida har bir darvoza ikki xil tranzistordan yasaladi:

  • pMOS: chiqishni ta'minot VDD ga (ya'ni "1" ga) ulaydi. Kirish 0 bo'lganda yopiladi (tok o'tkazadi).
  • nMOS: chiqishni yer GND ga (ya'ni "0" ga) ulaydi. Kirish 1 bo'lganda yopiladi.
Hayotiy o'xshatish: xonadagi suv baki ikki jo'mrakka ulangan: biri yuqoridagi to'la idishdan (VDD) suv quyadi, ikkinchisi pastga (GND) to'kadi. Tutqich shunday yasalganki, bir vaqtda faqat bittasi ochiq. Shuning uchun suv hech qachon yuqoridan pastga "teshib" oqib ketmaydi: idish tinch turganda suv (energiya) isrof bo'lmaydi.

Natija: tinch holatda VDD dan GND ga tok yo'q, ya'ni CMOS darvoza tinch turganda deyarli quvvat sarflamaydi. Energiya faqat almashishda (0 → 1 yoki 1 → 0) sarflanadi. Bu 5-bo'limdagi formulaning asosi.

Ulangan kalit ideal sim emas: uning kichik qarshiligi bor. Slaydda yuqori kalit qarshiligi ROH, pastkisi ROL (chiqish qarshiligi). Kalit sifatida ishlaganda MOS tranzistor triod (chiziqli) sohada bo'ladi: u yerda u oddiy kichik rezistordek ishlaydi.

081. A metal-oxide-semiconductor transistor works as a switch: a) in the triode region   b) in the saturation region   c) with forward-biased gate-source junction   d) with reverse-biased gate-source junction

Javob

a) in the triode region. Yopiq kalit = kichik qarshilik RON, bu triod soha. (MOS da gate izolyatsiyalangan, "junction" yo'q, shuning uchun c va d ma'nosiz.)

2. Kuchlanish sathlari: VOH, VOL, VIH, VIL

Haqiqiy darvoza chiqishi aniq 3.3 V yoki aniq 0 V bermaydi: tok olinsa, ROH/ROL da kuchlanish tushadi. Shuning uchun ishlab chiqaruvchi (datasheet) kafolat beradi (24-darsda VIL va VIH ni ko'rgandik):

BelgiKimnikiMa'nosi
VOHchiqish"1" berganda chiqish kamida shuncha
VOLchiqish"0" berganda chiqish ko'pi bilan shuncha
VIHkirishkirish shundan yuqori bo'lsa, "1" deb o'qiladi
VILkirishkirish shundan past bo'lsa, "0" deb o'qiladi
IOH, IOLchiqishVOH/VOL kafolatlangan eng katta chiqish toki

VIL va VIH orasidagi kirish kuchlanishi noaniq: darvoza uni 0 deb ham, 1 deb ham o'qishi mumkin.

Shovqin zaxirasi (noise margin)

NMH = VOH − VIH   ·   NML = VIL − VOL

Shovqin zaxirasi = signal yo'lda qancha "buzilsa" ham qabul qilgich hali to'g'ri o'qiydi. Misolda driver "1" uchun kamida 2.9 V beradi, qabul qilgichga esa 2.0 V yetarli: yo'lda 0.9 V gacha shovqin (pasayish) zarar qilmaydi.

O'xshatish: o'qituvchi "60 balldan yuqori = o'tdi" deydi (VIH). Siz doim kamida 75 ball olasiz (VOH). Oradagi 15 ball sizning zaxirangiz: biror savolda adashsangiz ham o'tasiz.

Ikki darvozani ulash mumkinmi? (static compatibility check)

Slayddagi 4 qadam:

  1. 0 va 1 holatini alohida tekshiring.
  2. Kuchlanish: VOL < VIL va VOH > VIH (ya'ni ikkala shovqin zaxirasi musbat).
  3. Chiqish tokini hisoblang: ulangan kirishlar toklari (CMOS da deyarli 0) + yuklama toklari (LED, rezistor).
  4. Tok: |IO| < |IOL| (0 holatda), |IO| < |IOH| (1 holatda). Tok ko'p bo'lsa, VOH/VOL kafolati yo'qoladi.

090. Which of the following conditions implies an incorrect connection between logic gates? a) VI < VIL   b) VI > VIH   c) IO < |IOL|   d) IO > |IOH|

Javob

d) IO > |IOH|. Chiqish ruxsat etilganidan ko'p tok beryapti → VOH kafolati buziladi. a va b oddiy to'g'ri holatlar (aniq 0 va aniq 1), c esa ruxsat etilgan tok.

2021-07 (test). The outputs of two identical open-drain logic gates are connected together. 2021-06 (test). The static power consumption of an open-drain CMOS inverter with the output at the low level: (a) Depends on the total capacitive load connected to the output. (b) Depends on the output pull-down resistance. (c) Depends on any resistive load connected to the output. (d) Does not depend on the external resistance between the output and ground (GND).

Javob

(d). Chiqish 0 V bo'lganda chiqishdan GND ga ulangan rezistorda kuchlanish 0, demak tok ham 0. Tok faqat VDD ga ulangan rezistordan (pull-up) keladi. Open-drain da pull-down rezistor yo'q (b noto'g'ri), sig'im faqat almashish paytida ta'sir qiladi (a noto'g'ri), har qanday rezistiv yuklama emas, faqat VDD ga ulangani (c noto'g'ri).

4. Quvvat sarfi: statik va dinamik

Statik quvvat PS: hech narsa almashmaganda ham sarflanadigan quvvat (kichik sizish toki Idc): PS = VDD · Idc. CMOS da u juda kichik, ko'p masalalarda "negligible" deyiladi.

Dinamik quvvat PD: chiqish 0 ↔ 1 almashganda yuklama sig'imi C ni zaryadlash va bo'shatish uchun sarflanadi. Yuklama sig'imi = keyingi darvozalar kirishlari (gate) + simlar sig'imi.

PD = f · C · V²

Bu yerda f = almashish chastotasi (takt), C = yuklama sig'imi, V = ta'minot kuchlanishi VDD. Xulosa (testlar shu!):

Nima o'zgardiPD qanday o'zgaradiSabab

C × ½× ½C ga to'g'ri proporsional
V × 2× 4V kvadratda
V × ½ (3.3 V → 1.65 V)× ¼shuning uchun zamonaviy mikrosxemalar past kuchlanishda (LV/LVC 3.3 V, 1.8 V …)

5. Kechikish (propagation delay): RC model

Chiqish sig'imi C kalit qarshiligi RO orqali zaryadlanadi (3-darsdagi RC zanjir). Kuchlanish birdaniga emas, asta o'sadi. Kechikish tP = kirish o'zgargan paytdan chiqish o'zgarishning 50% iga yetgan paytgacha vaqt (slayd: "at the 50 % of the whole range").

tP = 0.69 · RO · CL    (0.69 = ln 2: RC zanjir 50% ga yetish vaqti)
  • tP ∝ CL: yuklama sig'imi 2 marta → kechikish 2 marta.
  • Bitta chiqishga ko'p kirish ulansa, sig'imlar qo'shiladi: CT = Σ CIi → sekinlashadi.
  • Fanout = bitta chiqish nechta kirishni boshqara oladi. CMOS da kirish toki deyarli yo'q, shuning uchun fanout sig'im (tezlik) bilan cheklanadi, tok bilan emas.
  • Rise time / fall time (tr, tf) = signal 10% dan 90% gacha o'tish vaqti.

040. A CMOS logic gate drives a load of capacity C. By doubling the capacity (2 C), the propagation delay changes as: a) 2   b) 1/2   c) 1/4   d) 4

Javob

a) 2. tP = 0.69·RO·C, C ga to'g'ri proporsional. (Diqqat: quvvat ham C ga proporsional, lekin V ga emas, V² ga.)

2021-07 (test). The propagation time from input to output for a CMOS NAND gate with 2 inputs when it drives a load capacitance CL = 0.2 fF is tP = 200 ps. Which is the propagation time tP of the gate if it drives 2 inputs of 2 other CMOS NAND gates, which have a total gate capacitance CG = 0.8 fF? (a) 5 times higher. (b) depends on the pull-up resistor. (c) 800 ps. (d) 1.6 ns.

Javob

(c) 800 ps. Yangi yuklama 0.8 fF = eski 0.2 fF × 4 → tP × 4 = 800 ps (rasmiy yechim: "the propagation time increases four times when the load capacitance increases four times").

6. Imtihon masalasi: 2021-06 (7 ball)

A CMOS data processing circuit is made of 100 million MOS transistors. Each MOS transistor has a gate capacitance CG = 0.3 fF, drives on average the gates of 2 other MOS transistors, and is supplied at VDD = 1.8 V. The circuit can operate in two modes: idle mode, in which 1 % of all the transistors are active; active mode, in which 80 % of all the transistors are active. We assume that the active transistors in each operating mode are driven by signals with the frequency of the clock, Fclock = 100 MHz. Calculate the dynamic power dissipated by the processing circuit in the idle mode, Pidle (2 points), and in the active mode, Pactive (1 point).

Assuming that: the ambient temperature is 23 °C; the static power is negligible; the thermal resistance junction-case is ΘJC = 1.1 °C/W; the thermal resistance case-ambient is ΘCA = 43 °C/W; calculate the junction temperature for the circuit operating in the idle mode, TJidle (2 points).

If the supply voltage doubles, becoming VDD = 3.6 V, the dynamic power becomes (1 point): is unchanged / decreases to a half / decreases to a quarter / increases 2 times / increases 4 times. If the clock frequency doubles, becoming Fclock = 200 MHz, the dynamic power becomes (1 point): is unchanged / decreases to a half / increases 2 times / decreases to a quarter / increases 4 times.

Yechim (qadam-baqadam)
  1. Bitta tranzistor yuklamasi: u 2 ta gate ni boshqaradi → CL = 2 · CG = 2 · 0.3 fF = 0.6 fF = 0.6·10−15 F.
  2. Bittasining quvvati: f·CL·V² = 108 · 0.6·10−15 · 1.8² = 108 · 0.6·10−15 · 3.24 = 1.944·10−7 W.
  3. Idle: N = 1% · 108 = 106 ta → Pidle = 106 · 1.944·10−7 = 0.194 W.
  4. Active: N = 80% · 108 = 8·107 ta → Pactive = 8·107 · 1.944·10−7 = 15.6 W.
  5. Harorat: issiqlik qarshiliklari ketma-ket qo'shiladi: ΘJA = 1.1 + 43 = 44.1 °C/W. TJ = TA + ΘJA · P = 23 + 44.1 · 0.194 = 31.6 °C.
  6. VDD × 2 → increases 4 times. Fclock × 2 → increases 2 times.

Rasmiy formula: PD = N · Fclock · CL · V². "Issiqlik qarshiligi" haqida batafsil 30-darsda: TJ = TA + P·Θ, ya'ni harorat farqi = quvvat × issiqlik qarshiligi (Om qonuniga o'xshash: kuchlanish = tok × qarshilik).

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

NMH = VOH − VIH  ·  NML = VIL − VOL  ·  moslik: VOL < VIL, VOH > VIH, |IO| < |IOL|, |IOH| PD = N · f · C · V²  ·  PS = VDD · Idc  ·  tP = 0.69 · RO · CL
  • CMOS: pMOS → VDD, nMOS → GND, bir vaqtda bittasi ON → tinch holatda tok yo'q. MOS kalit = triod soha.
  • f × 2 → PD × 2; C × ½ → PD × ½; V × 2 → PD × 4; C × 2 → tP × 2.
  • Yuklama sig'imi = boshqariladigan gate lar yig'indisi (2021-06: CL = 2·CG).
  • Ikki chiqish (3-S yoki open-drain) ikkalasi 0 berib parallel → ROL ‖ ROL → VOUT ≤ VOL.
  • Open-drain, chiqish 0: GND ga ulangan rezistor tok olmaydi; tok faqat VDD ga ulangan rezistordan (pull-up).
  • IO > |IOH| (yoki |IOL|) = noto'g'ri ulanish. Fanout CMOS da sig'im bilan cheklanadi.
  • Issiqlik: ΘJA = ΘJC + ΘCA, TJ = TA + ΘJA · P.
