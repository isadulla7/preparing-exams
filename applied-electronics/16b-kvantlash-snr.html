16b. Kvantlash, desibel, SNR va ENOB

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: ADC qiymatni qanday yaxlitlashini (kvantlash), kerakli bitlar sonini hisoblash, desibel (dB) nima ekanini, signal/shovqin nisbati (SNR) formulalarini, ENOB ni va anti-aliasing filtrining qutblari sonini hisoblashni. Bazadagi 10 ta savol (016, 109, 049, 033, 042, 078, 019, 053, 045 va slayd mashqlari) shu yerda yechiladi. Manba: D1 slaydlarining ikkinchi yarmi. O'qish vaqti: 40 daqiqa. Avval 16a-darsni o'qing.

1. Kvantlash: qiymatni pog'onaga yaxlitlash

ADC kirishi bir oraliqda ishlaydi: 0 dan S gacha. S to'liq shkala (full scale, input range, input dynamic). Masalan, 0–10 V yoki ±1 V (bunda S = 2 V).

N bitli ADC bu oraliqni 2N ta teng bo'lakka bo'ladi va har bo'lakka bitta son (kod) beradi: 0 dan 2N − 1 gacha.

  • LSB (least significant bit, eng kichik pog'ona): bitta oraliqning kengligi. LSB = S / 2N.
  • Kvantlash xatosi εq (quantization error): haqiqiy qiymat bilan kod bildirgan qiymat orasidagi farq. U −½ LSB dan +½ LSB gacha bo'ladi.
LSB = S / 2N     |εq|max = LSB / 2 = S / 2N+1
Chizg'ich faqat santimetr bo'linmalariga ega bo'lsa, 7.3 sm ni "7" deb yozasiz. Xato eng ko'pi bilan yarim santimetr. Millimetrli chizg'ich (ko'proq bit) xatoni kamaytiradi.

Misol: S = 10 V, N = 8 → LSB = 10 / 256 ≈ 39 mV, maksimal xato ≈ 19.5 mV.

Kerakli bitlar sonini topish

Xato foizda (to'liq shkalaga nisbatan) beriladi. Nisbiy xato:

LSB bo'yicha: 1 / 2N     ±½ LSB bo'yicha: 1 / 2N+1
Diqqat, rasmiy yechimlarda ikki xil usul bor. 016-savolda xato = 1/2N (LSB) deb olingan. 109-savolda "bipolar quantization error" deyilgan va 1/2N+1 olingan. 049-savol yechimida ikkala javob ham to'g'ri deyilgan. Test savolida ikkala usul bilan hisoblang va qaysi natija variantlarda borligiga qarang.

016-savol

In an A/D conversion system, how many bits are needed to obtain a total error of less than 1.2%, assuming that half of this error is due to quantization?
a) 8   b) 9   c) 10   d) 7
  1. Kvantlash xatosi: 1.2% / 2 = 0.6% = 0.006.
  2. Rasmiy yechim: 1/2N ≤ 0.006 → 2N ≥ 100 / 0.6 ≈ 166.
  3. 27 = 128 yetmaydi, 28 = 256 yetadi → a) 8.

109-savol

A group of four analog signals has (for each channel) levels between –1 V and +1 V, maximum frequency Fmax = 2 kHz. The A/D converter has input dynamic from 0 V to +10 V. ... Calculate the minimum number of bits of the converter for a bipolar quantization error below 0.1%.
  1. Bipolyar (±½ LSB): 1/2N+1 < 0.001 → 2N+1 > 1000.
  2. 210 = 1024 → N + 1 = 10 → N = 9 bit (rasmiy javob).

049-savol

... Calculate the number of bits of the converter to have a quantization error less than 0.2%.

1/2N < 0.002 → 2N > 500 → N = 9. Rasmiy yechim: "8 is also good considering as relative error 1/2N+1" (29 = 512 > 500 → N = 8).

2. Desibel (dB) nima?

Elektronikada nisbatlar juda katta bo'lishi mumkin (1 000 000 baravar). Bunday sonlar bilan ishlash qulay bo'lishi uchun desibel (dB) ishlatiladi. dB nisbatni "nechta nol bor" degan tilga o'giradi.

Logarifm nima: lg(x) = log10(x) "10 ni necha marta ko'paytirsak x chiqadi" degani. lg(100) = 2, lg(1000) = 3. log2(x) esa "2 ni necha marta": log2(8) = 3, log2(256) = 8. Kalkulyatorda "log" tugmasi lg ni beradi.

Quvvatlar nisbati: dB = 10 · lg(P1/P2)     Kuchlanishlar (amplitudalar) nisbati: dB = 20 · lg(V1/V2)
Amplituda nisbatidB
2 baravar≈ 6 dB
10 baravar20 dB
100 baravar40 dB
256 baravar (8 bit)20 · lg 256 ≈ 48 dB
1000 baravar60 dB

Eslab qolish uchun: amplituda 2 baravar = 6 dB. Shuning uchun keyinroq "1 bit = 6 dB" chiqadi: har qo'shimcha bit pog'onani 2 baravar kichraytiradi.

dB dan orqaga o'girish: x dB → amplituda nisbati 10x/20, quvvat nisbati 10x/10. Masalan, 40 dB → 102 = 100 baravar amplituda.

3. SNR: signal va shovqin nisbati

SNR (signal-to-noise ratio, signal/shovqin nisbati) foydali signal quvvati shovqin quvvatidan necha baravar kattaligini dB da ko'rsatadi. Katta SNR yaxshi.

Shovqinli xonada gaplashish: siz qanchalik baland gapirsangiz (signal) va atrof qanchalik jim bo'lsa (shovqin), sizni shunchalik yaxshi eshitishadi. SNR shu "eshitilish" darajasi.

Kvantlash xatosi tasodifiy shovqin kabi ta'sir qiladi. Undan kelib chiqadigan nisbat SNRq (kvantlash SNR). Slaydda signal shakliga qarab uchta formula bor (signal to'liq shkalani egallasa):

Signal shakliSNRq
Uchburchak (triangular)6·N dB
Sinus (sinusoidal)6·N + 1.76 dB
Ovoz (voice, Gauss taqsimoti)6·N − 4.77 dB
  • Har bit +6 dB qo'shadi. 2 bit qo'shsak, SNRq 12 dB ga ortadi (slayddagi takrorlash savoli).
  • Formulalar signal to'liq shkalani egallaganda to'g'ri. Signal amplitudasi 2 baravar kichik bo'lsa, SNRq 6 dB ga kamayadi (10 baravar kichik bo'lsa 20 dB ga). Signal S dan katta bo'lsa, ADC to'yinadi (overload) va SNRq juda tez yomonlashadi.

"Uchburchak signal, SNR = 45 dB. Kvantlash SNR undan yaxshi bo'lishi uchun nechta bit kerak?" Uchburchak: 6N ≥ 45 → N ≥ 7.5 → N = 8. (Masalaning davomi, ADC turi va tezligi, 18-darsda.)

4. ENOB: haqiqatda nechta bit ishlayapti

Haqiqiy tizimda kvantlashdan tashqari boshqa xatolar ham bor (aliasing, kuchaytirgich shovqini, jitter). Shuning uchun o'lchangan umumiy SNR ideal 6N + 1.76 dan kichik bo'ladi. Shu umumiy SNR ga mos keladigan "foydali" bitlar soni ENOB (effective number of bits):

ENOB = (SNR − 1.76) / 6.02
10 megapikselli kamera, lekin linzasi xira. Surat aslida 8 megapikselli kameranikidek chiqadi. ADC "10 bitli" bo'lsa ham, shovqin tufayli faqat 8 biti foydali bo'lishi mumkin.

5. Aliasing SNR va anti-aliasing filtri

16a-darsdan: Fmax dan yuqoridagi chastotalar birinchi nusxa sifatida foydali bandga tushadi. Eng xavflisi Fs − Fmax chastotadagi qism: u aynan band chetiga (Fmax ga) tushadi. Filtr shu chastotada signalni qanchalik so'ndirsa, SNRa (aliasing SNR) shuncha bo'ladi.

Filtrning keskinligi qutblar soni (poles, filtr tartibi) bilan belgilanadi. Har bir qutb:

1 qutb = 20 dB/dekada = 6 dB/oktava
  • Dekada: chastota 10 baravar oshishi. Dekadalar soni = lg(f2/f1).
  • Oktava: chastota 2 baravar oshishi. Oktavalar soni = log2(f2/f1).
SNRa = Np · 20 · lg((Fs − Fmax)/Fmax) = Np · 6 · log2((Fs − Fmax)/Fmax) Np = SNRa / [20 · lg((Fs − Fmax)/Fmax)]  →  yuqoriga yaxlitlanadi

019-savol

Which of the variations below does not affect the aliasing signal/noise ratio (SNRa)?
a) Low pass filter on the input signal   b) Increase of the A/D sampling rate   c) Increase of the number of A/D bits   d) High pass filter on the input signal

SNRa filtrga (a, d) va Fs ga (b) bog'liq. Bitlar soni faqat kvantlash xatosiga ta'sir qiladi → c).

Slayddagi Exercise 1 (davomi): filtr qutblari

16a-darsdagi tizim: 8 bit, har kanal Fs = 25 kHz, band 5 kHz, "aliasing SNR 3 dB higher than the quantization SNR".

  1. SNRq = 6 · 8 + 1.76 = 49.76 dB → SNRa = 49.76 + 3 = 52.76 dB.
  2. Birinchi alias: 25 − 5 = 20 kHz. Nisbat 20/5 = 4 = 2 oktava → har qutb 2 · 6 = 12 dB beradi.
  3. Np = 52.76 / 12 = 4.4 → 5 qutb.

045-savol

An A/D conversion system has two input channels. On each channel there is a bipolar signal with effective value 50 mVeff and a frequency band 0→8 MHz. The A/D converter has an input dynamic of ±1 V. We have also a S/H circuit with the acquisition time (Tacq) of 4 ns. Indicate the requirements of the anti-aliasing filter for an aliasing error equal to the maximum quantization error.
  1. Filtrning kesish chastotasi: band chegarasi, 8 MHz.
  2. 16a-darsdagi 076 dan: har kanal 64 MS/s → birinchi alias 64 − 8 = 56 MHz.
  3. Kvantlash xatosi 1/256 (8 bit, 108-savoldagi kabi) → filtr 56 MHz da 256 baravar so'ndirishi kerak: 20 · lg 256 = 48 dB.
  4. 8 dan 56 MHz gacha: lg(56/8) = lg 7 = 0.845 dekada.
  5. Kerakli keskinlik: 48 / 0.845 = 56.8 dB/dek. 2 qutb (40) yetmaydi, 3 qutb (60 dB/dek) yetadi.

6. Umumiy SNR

Har xil xatolar bir-biriga bog'liq emas, shuning uchun ularning quvvatlari qo'shiladi. Slayddagi formula:

SNRt = −10 · lg(10−SNR1/10 + 10−SNR2/10 + ...)

Natija doim eng yomon (eng kichik) SNR dan ham kichikroq chiqadi.

053-savol

A four-channel acquisition system uses an eight-bit tracking A/D converter with 100 MHz clock frequency and a Sample & Hold circuit with 1 μs acquisition time. For each channel, we have a sampling frequency Fs = 70 kHz, the maximum frequency of the signal Fmax = 23 kHz, and an eight-pole anti-aliasing filter. Calculate: the quantization signal-to-noise ratio for sinusoidal signals, SNRq; the aliasing signal-to-noise ratio, SNRa; and the total signal-to-noise ratio, SNRtot.
  1. SNRq = 6 · 8 + 1.76 ≈ 50 dB.
  2. (Fs − Fmax)/Fmax = (70 − 23)/23 ≈ 2 = 1 oktava → SNRa = 8 qutb · 6 dB · 1 = 48 dB.
  3. Rasmiy yechim: SNRtot = −20 · lg(10−48/20 + 10−50/20) = −20 · lg(0.00398 + 0.00316) ≈ 43 dB.
053 ning rasmiy yechimi xato amplitudalarini qo'shgan (eng yomon holat) va 43 dB olgan. Slayddagi quvvat formulasi bilan 45.9 dB chiqadi. Imtihon savollari shu bazadan olinadi, shuning uchun 053 kabi savolda 43 dB ni bering, yoki ikkala usulni ham yozib, qaysi biri ekanini ko'rsating.

📝 Konspekt (daftarga yozib oling)

Kvantlash

LSB = S/2N  ·  |εq| ≤ LSB/2 = S/2N+1
  • Bitlar: 1/2N ≤ xato (016) yoki bipolyar 1/2N+1 ≤ xato (109). Ikkalasini hisoblab, variantga qarang.

dB

  • Quvvat: 10·lg, amplituda: 20·lg. ×2 = 6 dB, ×10 = 20 dB, ×256 = 48 dB.

SNR

Uchburchak 6N · Sinus 6N + 1.76 · Ovoz 6N − 4.77 (dB) ENOB = (SNR − 1.76)/6.02
  • +1 bit = +6 dB. Amplituda yarim → −6 dB.
  • Umumiy: SNRt = −10·lg Σ10−SNRi/10 (slayd); 053 rasmiy yechimi −20·lg Σ10−SNRi/20 → 43 dB.

Anti-aliasing filtri

SNRa = Np · 20·lg((Fs − Fmax)/Fmax)  ·  1 qutb = 20 dB/dek = 6 dB/okt
  • Bitlar soni SNRa ga ta'sir qilmaydi (019).
  • Javoblar: 016 → 8; 109 → 9; 049 → 9 (yoki 8); 033 → 8; 042, 078 → 9; Ex.1 → 5 qutb; 045 → 48 dB, 3 qutb; 053 → 50, 48, 43 dB.
O'qib bo'lgach chatga yozing: "tushundim" yoki "tushunmadim" (qaysi joyi qiyinligini ayting).
