18. ADC turlari: flash, ketma-ket yaqinlashish (SAR), tracking

Bu darsda nimani bilib olasiz: ADC ichida nima bo'lishini va uning uch asosiy turini: flash (parallel), ketma-ket yaqinlashish (SAR) va tracking/zinapoya. Har birining tezligi (o'zgartirish vaqti) va murakkabligi (komparatorlar soni) qanday hisoblanishini. Bu A mavzu: 2020-07 imtihon masalasi, 2021-06 va 2021-07 imtihonlarining testlari, bazadagi 013, 035, 108-savollar shu yerda. Manba: D3 slaydlari. O'qish vaqti: 35 daqiqa.

1. Asosiy g'isht: komparator

Komparator (comparator, solishtirgich) ikki kuchlanishni solishtiradi va javob beradi: birinchisi katta bo'lsa 1, kichik bo'lsa 0. Bu 1 bitli "ADC".

Tarozi: chap pallasi og'irroqmi yoki o'ngi? Javob faqat "ha" yoki "yo'q". Har qanday ADC shunday tarozilardan yasaladi, faqat ularni qancha va qanday tartibda ishlatishi bilan farq qiladi.

Har bir ADC ni ikki o'lchov bilan baholaymiz (slayd):

  • Murakkablik (complexity): nechta komparator kerak. Kam bo'lsa arzon va oddiy.
  • O'zgartirish vaqti Tc (conversion time): bitta sonni olish uchun nechta takt (komparator ishlashi) kerak. Kam bo'lsa tez.

Qoida: tez ADC murakkab, oddiy ADC sekin. Ham tez, ham ko'p bitli ADC qimmat.

2. Flash (parallel) ADC: hammasini birdaniga

Rezistorlar zanjiri VR kuchlanishni teng pog'onalarga bo'ladi (2-darsdagi kuchlanish bo'lgich). Har pog'onaga bitta komparator. Kirish A hamma chegaralar bilan bir vaqtda solishtiriladi.

  • Komparatorlar soni: 2N − 1. 3 bit → 7, 8 bit → 255, 10 bit → 1023.
  • O'zgartirish vaqti: bitta solishtirish. N ga ham, kirish qiymatiga ham bog'liq emas.
  • Eng tez, lekin eng murakkab. Bit qo'shilsa komparatorlar soni 2 baravar oshadi.
Imtihon natijasini e'lon qilish: 255 ta o'qituvchi bir vaqtda "sizning ballingiz meniki chegaradan yuqorimi?" deb tekshiradi. Bir zumda javob, lekin 255 ta odam kerak.

Imtihon testi: 2021-07

The conversion time of a flash ADC:
(a) Is proportional with the number of bits on output, N.
(b) Is independent on the input signal level.
(c) Depends as 2N on the number of the bits in output, N.
(d) Depends as log N on the number of the bits in output, N.

Flash hamma solishtirishni parallel bajaradi, vaqt N ga ham, signal sathiga ham bog'liq emas. Javob (b). (2N ga bog'liq narsa komparatorlar soni, vaqt emas.)

3. DAC orqali qayta aloqa: bitta komparator yetadi

Boshqa ADC lar bitta komparator bilan ishlaydi. Mantiq sxemasi taxmin D ni yasaydi, DAC (17-dars) uni kuchlanish A' ga aylantiradi, komparator A va A' ni solishtiradi. Mantiq javobga qarab D ni to'g'rilaydi va A' A ga yetguncha takrorlaydi. D ni qanday o'zgartirishiga qarab ikki usul bor.

3a. Zinapoya (staircase) va tracking ADC: 1 LSB qadamlar

  • Zinapoya ADC: hisoblagich 0 dan boshlab har taktda 1 ga oshadi. A' > A bo'lganda to'xtaydi. Har yangi o'zgartirishdan oldin 0 ga qaytariladi.
  • Tracking (kuzatuvchi) ADC: yuqoriga-pastga sanaydigan hisoblagich. A' < A bo'lsa +1, A' > A bo'lsa −1. Qayta 0 ga tushirish kerak emas: signalni doim "kuzatib" yuradi.
Tc,max = 2N · Tck = 2N / fck    (0 dan to'liq shkalagacha yurish)

Tracking ADC kirish tezligini kuzata olishi uchun signal bir taktda 1 LSB dan ko'p o'zgarmasligi kerak:

|dV/dt|max ≤ LSB · fck     sinus uchun: 2π·f·A ≤ LSB · fck

Bu yerda A sinusning amplitudasi (cho'qqidan cho'qqigacha kattalikning yarmi), 2π·f·A esa sinusning eng katta o'zgarish tezligi.

  • Bitta komparator: eng oddiy. Lekin eng sekin: 2N gacha takt.
Zinapoyadan yuqoriga bittalab ko'tarilish: 256 qavatli binoda 256 qadam. Lift yo'q, lekin oyoqdan boshqa hech narsa kerak emas.

3b. Ketma-ket yaqinlashish (SAR) ADC: ikkiga bo'lib qidirish

SAR (successive approximation register) eng katta bitdan (MSB) boshlab har taktda bitta bitni aniqlaydi:

  1. A ni S/2 bilan solishtiradi. A katta bo'lsa MSB = 1, aks holda 0. Shu bilan oraliqning yarmi tashlab yuboriladi.
  2. Qolgan yarimning o'rtasi bilan solishtiradi (¾S yoki ¼S) va keyingi bitni oladi.
  3. Shunday davom etadi. N bit uchun aynan N qadam.
"1 dan 100 gacha son o'yladim, top" o'yini. Eng yaxshi usul: "50 dan kattami?", "75 dan kattami?" ... Har savol variantlarni yarmiga kamaytiradi, 7 savolda topasiz. Bittalab so'rash (1? 2? 3?) esa 100 tagacha savol oladi: bu tracking.
Tc = N · Tck    (har doim, kirish qiymatidan qat'i nazar)

SAR to'g'ri ishlashi uchun kirish butun o'zgartirish vaqti (N takt) davomida 1 LSB dan ko'p o'zgarmasligi kerak (yoki oldida S/H bo'ladi). Sinus uchun:

2π·f·A ≤ LSB · fck / N

Imtihon testi: 2021-06

The conversion time of a successive approximation analog-to-digital converter with an N-bit output is proportional to:
(a) 2N − 1.   (b) The slew rate of the signal in overload.   (c) The value of the signal (faster for lower values).   (d) The number of output bits.

SAR har taktda bitta bit chiqaradi → vaqt bitlar soniga proporsional. Javob (d).

5. Masalalar

Slayddagi Exercise 1: tracking ADC

An 8-bit tracking ADC receives in input a sinusoidal signal of 1 V p-p. The ADC clock is 1 MHz. The DAC of the ADC has LSB = 10 mV. Calculate: max frequency of the input sine wave that avoids the ADC overload; max conversion time (for a step signal 0 V → full scale).
  1. 1 V p-p (cho'qqidan cho'qqigacha) → amplituda A = 0.5 V.
  2. 2π·f·0.5 ≤ 0.01 · 106 → f ≤ 10 000 / (2π · 0.5) ≈ 3.18 kHz.
  3. Tc,max = 28 / 1 MHz = 256 μs.

Slayddagi Exercise 2: SAR ADC

A successive approximation 8-bit ADC receives in input a sinusoidal signal of 1 V p-p. The ADC clock is 1 MHz, and the DAC has LSB = 10 mV. Calculate: max conversion time; max input signal frequency that can be converted without errors.
  1. Tc = 8 / 1 MHz = 8 μs.
  2. 2π·f·0.5 ≤ 0.01 · 106 / 8 → f ≤ 1250 / π ≈ 398 Hz.

SAR tezroq bo'lsa ham, S/H siz u faqat sekin signallarni xatosiz o'zgartiradi. Shuning uchun amalda oldiga S/H qo'yiladi (16a-dars).

108-savol: qaysi ADC turi?

An A/D conversion system has two input channels ... frequency band 0→8 MHz ... S/H acquisition time 4 ns. The converter must operate on 8 bits. We have comparators with Tcomp = 2 ns. Indicate what ADC type can be used. Draw the (partial) block diagram for that ADC. Evaluate the complexity of the ADC.
  1. 16a-darsdagi 076 dan: Tconv ≤ 3.8 ns.
  2. Bitta komparator 2 ns ishlaydi. 3.8 ns ga faqat bitta solishtirish sig'adi (SAR ga 8 × 2 = 16 ns kerak edi).
  3. Demak faqat flash ADC. Murakkabligi: 28 − 1 = 255 komparator. Blok sxema: yuqoridagi flash rasmi, faqat 255 komparator bilan.

013-savol: tracking ADC li 4 kanalli tizim

A four-channel acquisition system uses an eight-bit tracking A/D converter with 100 MHz clock frequency and a Sample & Hold circuit with 1 μs acquisition time. For each channel, we want to have a sampling frequency Fs equal to three times the maximum frequency Fmax of the signal and an eight-pole anti-aliasing filter. Determine the maximum frequency per channel Fmax.
  1. Tracking, eng yomon holat: Tconv = 28 / 100 MHz = 2.56 μs.
  2. Bitta namuna: 2.56 + 1 = 3.56 μs → Fconv = 1 / 3.56 μs ≈ 280 kHz.
  3. 4 kanal: har biriga 280 / 4 = 70 kHz.
  4. Fs = 3 · Fmax → Fmax = 70 / 3 ≈ 23.3 kHz.

Shu tizimning SNR lari 16b-darsdagi 053-savolda hisoblangan.

035-savol: tracking ADC uchun maksimal bitlar

... The A/D converter is a tracking circuit with input dynamics from 0 V to +10 V. Draw the block schematic of the tracking A/D converter. If the maximum conversion time of the A/D converter is 8.5 μs, specify the maximum number of bits of the converter running using a clock frequency of 125 MHz.
  1. 8.5 μs ichida taktlar soni: 125 · 106 · 8.5 · 10−6 = 1062.
  2. Tracking ga 2N takt kerak: 2N ≤ 1062. 210 = 1024 ≤ 1062, 211 = 2048 juda ko'p → N = 10.

Blok sxema: komparator (A va A' ni solishtiradi) → yuqoriga/pastga hisoblagich (U/D, takt bilan) → DAC → A' komparatorga qaytadi; hisoblagich chiqishi D.

2020-07 imtihoni, 2-masala (7 ball)

An analog to digital converter (ADC) is used to convert a triangular wave analog signal with a frequency of 35 Hz, an amplitude that covers the full scale of the ADC, and a signal to noise ratio (SNR) of 45 dB. The ADC minimum sampling rate must be at least 20 times higher than the signal frequency. Calculate the maximum ADC sampling frequency for the least complex ADC architecture that operates with a clock frequency of 200 kHz and with a signal to quantization noise ratio higher than the SNR.
  1. "Least complex" (eng oddiy) → tracking ADC, Tc = 2N takt.
  2. Uchburchak signal: SNRq = 6N > 45 dB → N ≥ 7.5 → N = 8 (16b-dars).
  3. 28 = 256 takt → Fs = 200 kHz / 256 = 781.25 Hz.
  4. Tekshiruv: 20 · 35 = 700 Hz < 781.25 Hz. Shart bajarildi.

Rasmiy javob: 781.25 Hz.

O'zingizni tekshiring

12 bitli SAR, fck = 24 MHz. Tc?
Javob12 / 24 MHz = 0.5 μs.

Formulalar

Tracking: 2π·f·A ≤ LSB·fck  ·  SAR: 2π·f·A ≤ LSB·fck/N Fs,max = 1/(Tacq + Tc)  ·  tracking bitlari: 2N ≤ fck·Tc,max

Esda tuting

  • "Least complex" → tracking. "Fastest" yoki Tc juda kichik → flash.
  • Uchburchak: 6N; sinus: 6N + 1.76 → N ni toping, keyin takt sonini.
  • Javoblar: 2021-07 test → (b); 2021-06 test → (d); 108 → flash, 255 komparator; 013 → 23.3 kHz; 035 → 10 bit; 2020-07 → 781.25 Hz; Ex.1 → 3.18 kHz, 256 μs; Ex.2 → 8 μs, 398 Hz.
